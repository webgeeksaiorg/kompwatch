# How Do I Use Competitive Intelligence to Onboard and Ramp New Sales Reps Faster?

**Short answer:** Give new reps a "competitive snapshot" briefing in week one — who the top 3 competitors are, what's changed in the last 30 days, and the one-sentence counter for each. KompWatch's change history and digest archive make that briefing something you can generate in 15 minutes, not a week-long project.

---

## Why CI Is a Ramp Accelerant

The biggest gap in most sales onboarding programs isn't product knowledge — it's competitive fluency. New reps learn what KompWatch does from the demo script. What they don't learn until they lose their first deal: what Klue, Kompyte, or Crayon is saying about themselves right now, what their pricing looks like this quarter, and what objections they'll hear in week three.

A CI-for-onboarding workflow fixes this. The goal isn't to make new reps competitive intelligence analysts — it's to give them enough context to handle the top 3 competitor objections on day one and enough live data awareness to stay current on their own.

---

## The Week-One CI Briefing (15 Minutes to Prepare)

For each of your top 2–3 competitors, use KompWatch's 30-day change history to generate a quick brief:

1. **Go to [/competitors](https://kompwatch.com/competitors)** → select a competitor → filter to last 30 days, MEDIUM or higher severity
2. **Note the 2–3 most significant changes** — pricing adjustments, feature launches, messaging rewrites
3. **Pull the AI summary** — each KompWatch change entry includes a one-line summary and a "strategic implication" already written by the AI

You now have a brief that answers: *"What is this competitor doing right now, and what should a new rep know?"*

Format it as a one-pager per competitor:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Current positioning** | Screenshot or summary of their current homepage hero |
| **Pricing** | Current tiers and prices from their /pricing page, pulled this week |
| **Last 30 days** | 2–3 changes from KompWatch (AI summary, severity) |
| **Top 3 objections + counters** | From your existing battlecard |
| **When they tend to come up** | Deal stage, ICP, use case |

## Building a "Competitive Playbook" From Change History

KompWatch's change timeline is a historical record, not just a live alert. New reps can use it to pattern-match:

- **6-month pricing history** — Has this competitor raised prices? Shifted tiers? Added a free tier? Knowing the trajectory tells reps whether to expect a price-match play.
- **Feature launch cadence** — Do they ship frequently or go quiet for months? A quiet period often means a big launch is coming.
- **Messaging drift** — Has their homepage hero changed 4 times in 6 months? That's a company that hasn't found product-market fit on positioning — a ramp rep can learn to apply pressure on that inconsistency.

Go to [/competitors/[id]](https://kompwatch.com/competitors) → **View history** to access the full change timeline with timestamps.

## Keeping Reps Current After Ramp

Onboarding CI doesn't end at week one. The habit to build:

- **Subscribe new reps to digest emails** — weekly digest gives them a passive update without requiring them to check a dashboard. Set up in Settings → Digest → Add recipients.
- **Route HIGH/CRITICAL changes to #sales Slack** — reps see the alert in context of their daily workflow, not buried in a separate tool.
- **Include a "competitive update" in weekly team standup** — use the KompWatch weekly digest as the agenda item. 5 minutes, not a deck.

---

## Common Mistake: Giving Reps Raw Digests

A 12-item digest email forwarded to a new rep is not a competitive brief — it's homework they won't do. The effective approach is to curate a brief for onboarding (one-pager per competitor, current week) and then move to automated digest once they know the competitive landscape well enough to contextualize the alerts.
