# Is Kompyte Enterprise-Only Now?

As of mid-2026, Kompyte — the competitive intelligence tool formerly available as a Semrush add-on — is being positioned and marketed exclusively as an enterprise product. If you're a small team, startup, or mid-market company that relied on Kompyte through a Semrush Business subscription, this is what you need to know.

## What Changed?

When Adobe acquired Semrush in February 2026, the entire Semrush product portfolio — including Kompyte — moved under Adobe's go-to-market umbrella. Adobe's core business is large enterprise marketing contracts (Adobe Experience Cloud, Marketo, Adobe Analytics). The result: Kompyte is now marketed as an enterprise-tier solution, consistent with Adobe's standard positioning.

Practically speaking:

- **Self-serve access to Kompyte via Semrush plans is being phased out or made unavailable** for non-enterprise tiers.
- **New Kompyte contracts require a sales engagement** — no self-serve signup, no monthly billing, no trial without talking to an enterprise sales rep.
- **Pricing is now sales-negotiated**, not publicly listed. Reports from Vendr and procurement teams indicate enterprise minimums are in the $30,000–$60,000/yr range.

## Does This Affect My Existing Semrush Subscription?

If you're mid-contract: your Kompyte access should remain through your current term. The risk is at renewal.

Adobe is unlikely to offer the same self-serve, bundled-at-a-reasonable-price Kompyte access that you had under the old Semrush. When your contract comes up, expect:

- An upsell to an Adobe enterprise agreement to keep Kompyte access
- Or Kompyte quietly disappearing from your renewal package

This is the same pattern that played out when Semrush acquired Kompyte from users of the original standalone product in 2022 — tiered access shrank, and the most useful features were locked behind higher-priced plans.
