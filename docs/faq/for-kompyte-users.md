# KompWatch for Kompyte Users

Kompyte is now three acquisitions deep: Semrush acquired Kompyte in 2022, then Adobe acquired Semrush for $1.9B in February 2026. As of mid-2026, Kompyte has been formally repositioned as enterprise-only — self-serve access via Semrush Business plans is being phased out, and new contracts require an enterprise sales engagement. If you're evaluating alternatives, this page explains how KompWatch compares and whether switching makes sense.

## I Already Have Kompyte Through Semrush — Why Would I Switch to KompWatch?

When you signed up for Semrush Business, you got Kompyte bundled in. That didn't make it free — you were paying for the full Semrush suite. Now Adobe owns Semrush, Kompyte is buried three layers deep inside an enterprise creative-software company, and the self-serve path is gone.

The realistic outcomes for Kompyte over the next two years: folded deeper into an Adobe Marketing Cloud bundle at enterprise-repriced rates, quietly deprioritized as a non-core Adobe feature, or deprecated when your current contract comes up for renewal.

KompWatch is a standalone, focused CI tool. It does one thing — monitors competitor websites and explains what changed — and is priced for SaaS teams, not enterprise suites. No sales call, no annual contract, free tier available immediately.

## Does KompWatch Replace the Full Semrush Suite?

No — KompWatch replaces the Kompyte competitor-monitoring workflow specifically, not Semrush's SEO, PPC, or .Trends traffic-intelligence modules. Most teams that switch use KompWatch for website change monitoring and keep a separate tool (Ahrefs, SE Ranking, Similarweb) for keyword research and traffic data.

## What Happens to My Kompyte Data?

KompWatch cannot import competitor lists or change history from Kompyte. Your monitoring history starts fresh when you add competitors.

Before switching, export any important historical changes or battlecard content from Kompyte — especially if you've built internal reports from it.

## How Do I Switch?

1. **Sign up at [kompwatch.com](https://kompwatch.com)** — free, no credit card required on the Free plan.
2. **Add the same competitor URLs** you tracked in Kompyte. See [Adding a Competitor](./adding-competitors.md).
3. **Set CSS selectors** to target specific sections — pricing table, feature comparison, changelog. This reduces noise significantly vs. Kompyte's full-page monitoring. See [CSS Selector Targeting](./css-selectors.md).
4. **Connect Slack** in Settings → Integrations if you use a `#competitive-intel` channel.

Your first snapshot runs immediately. Change detection starts after the second snapshot — typically within 24 hours on Free or a few hours on Pro.

## Can I Try KompWatch Without Canceling Semrush?

Yes. KompWatch's free tier (2 competitors, no credit card) lets you run both tools in parallel. Add your two most important competitors to KompWatch and compare digest quality against what Kompyte surfaces before committing.

## What If I'm Mid-Contract With Semrush?

You can start a KompWatch Free account immediately, even under an active Semrush contract. If you upgrade, KompWatch is $49/mo (Pro) or $149/mo (Team) — month-to-month, cancel anytime.
