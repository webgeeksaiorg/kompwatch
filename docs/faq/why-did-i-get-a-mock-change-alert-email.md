# Why Did I Get an Email With a Mock Competitor Change Alert?

About **24 hours after you sign up**, KompWatch sends a one-time **proof-of-value email** that shows you exactly what a real competitor change alert looks like — before you've necessarily seen one in the wild.

The email uses a clearly labeled example competitor ("Acme Rival") with three mock changes:

- A **pricing hike** (HIGH severity)
- A **feature launch** (MEDIUM severity)
- A **hiring surge** (LOW severity)

These are not real changes. The email is labeled as an example/demo so there's no confusion.

---

## Why Send a Mock Alert at All?

Many new users add a competitor but never see a real change alert during their first few days — either because their competitors haven't updated anything yet, or because the 24-hour snapshot cycle hasn't completed twice yet.

Without ever seeing what a real alert looks like, it's hard to know whether KompWatch is worth upgrading to. This email delivers the "aha moment" upfront:

> *This is what you'll get the moment a competitor changes their pricing, launches a new feature, or starts hiring aggressively.*

---

## Will I Also Get Real Alerts?

Yes — separately. The mock email is a one-time onboarding email. Your real competitor change alerts arrive once KompWatch has taken at least two snapshots of your tracked competitors and detected a meaningful difference. See [What Triggers My First Change Alert Email?](what-triggers-my-first-change-alert-email.md) for timing details.

---

## I Only Want Real Alerts — Can I Opt Out of Mock Emails?

The proof-of-value email is sent once per account and does not repeat. You don't need to opt out — it's already done.

If you'd like to stop **all** onboarding emails (including the nurture sequence), email [support@kompwatch.com](mailto:support@kompwatch.com) with your account email and we'll remove you immediately.

---

## Is This Email Legitimate?

Yes. KompWatch proof-of-value emails are sent from `hello@kompwatch.com`. Every link in the email points to `kompwatch.com`. We never ask for passwords, payment details, or personal information via email.

If anything looks suspicious, forward it to [support@kompwatch.com](mailto:support@kompwatch.com).
