## What the Template Includes

The template covers 9 sections per competitor, designed to fit on a single page:

| Section | What goes here |
|---------|---------------|
| **1. Snapshot** | What they do, stage, funding, positioning headline |
| **2. Pricing** | Tiers, prices, free trial status — with source + date verified |
| **3. ICP + Positioning** | Who they target, what they optimize for, what they de-prioritize |
| **4. Strengths** | Honest 1–5 scoring across 5 dimensions |
| **5. Weaknesses** | Specific, not generic ("$28K contract" not just "expensive") |
| **6. Win / Loss patterns** | Two sentences each — pulled from actual recent deals |
| **7. Objection responses** | Top 3 objections per competitor + crisp responses |
| **8. One-line summary** | Under 20 words — what your rep says on a live call |
| **9. Change log** | Dated row every time the analysis is updated |

## How Many Competitors Should I Analyze?

Three. Maybe four. Start with the names that come up in your last 20 lost-deal notes. Twelve shallow analyses lose to three deep ones every time.

---

## How Do I Keep It Current?

The template is designed to be maintained — not just written once. The critical step is automating the update trigger:

1. Add each competitor's **pricing page**, **features page**, and **homepage** to KompWatch
2. When any of those pages change, you get a digest with a plain-English summary of what changed
3. Review the relevant section and update it — takes about 5 minutes per change
4. Log the update in Section 9 so your team sees the doc is being maintained

Without automated monitoring, most competitive analyses go stale within 60 days. This is the step teams skip.

KompWatch's **free plan** monitors 2 competitors. **Pro** monitors up to 10.

---

## Is This Different from a Battlecard?

Yes. The competitive analysis is the full reference document (all 9 sections). A battlecard is a compressed one-page version reps use on live calls — mostly sections 7 and 8 above.

Same underlying data, different formats for different consumption contexts. See also: [Free Competitive Battlecard Template](/faq/free-battlecard-template).
