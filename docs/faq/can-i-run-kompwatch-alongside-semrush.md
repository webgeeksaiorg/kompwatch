# Can I Run KompWatch Alongside Semrush During Evaluation?

Yes — and this is the recommended way to switch. The Free plan gives you 2 competitors with no credit card required, so you can run KompWatch in parallel with your existing Semrush or Kompyte workflow before committing.

## Why Run Both at the Same Time?

If you're mid-contract with Semrush, you're not stuck waiting. Running both tools simultaneously lets you:

1. **Compare signal quality directly** — add the same 2–3 competitor URLs to KompWatch that you track in Semrush/Kompyte. After two weeks, compare the change summaries side-by-side. You'll quickly see which tool surfaces more actionable intel.
2. **Evaluate the digest format** — KompWatch's AI digests are plain-English summaries written at the level of "what does this mean for our positioning or roadmap?" Semrush's change alerts tend to be visual diff screenshots or raw content diffs. The format difference is significant.
3. **Time your cancellation** — Semrush annual contracts auto-renew. You can cancel 30+ days before your renewal date without penalty. Running KompWatch now gives you time to make the decision well before your Semrush renewal comes up.

2 competitors is enough to run a real evaluation — pick your two most-watched rivals.

## When Should I Upgrade to Pro?

If you're replacing Semrush's Kompyte add-on for your whole team's CI workflow, upgrade to Pro ($49/mo) before you cancel Semrush. Pro includes:

- Up to 20 competitors
- Daily AI digests (Smart or Daily mode)
- Slack webhook integration
- 6-month snapshot history

You'll want at least 2 full weeks of Pro data — covering one full monitoring cycle per competitor — before cancelling Semrush, so you're not flying blind during the transition.

## Do I Need to Export Anything from Semrush Before I Cancel?

Yes — before cancelling:

- **Export battlecard content** you've built in Kompyte/Semrush if any. KompWatch cannot import historical battlecard data.
- **Export competitor change history** if you use it for internal reporting. Your Semrush history disappears when the account closes.
- **Note your competitor URLs and selectors** — you'll re-add them in KompWatch manually (Settings → Competitors → Add).

Your KompWatch monitoring history starts from the date you add each competitor, not from any historical Semrush data.

## I'm Still Under Contract with Semrush — Can I Get a Refund If I Cancel Early?

Semrush's refund policy varies by plan. KompWatch can't process Semrush refunds, and we'd recommend checking directly with Semrush support before cancelling mid-contract.

If the contract issue is a barrier to switching, consider starting on KompWatch Free now and upgrading at your Semrush renewal date. Either way, your KompWatch account is waiting — you've already started building monitoring history.
