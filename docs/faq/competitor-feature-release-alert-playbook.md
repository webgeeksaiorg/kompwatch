# What Do I Do When I Get a Competitor Feature-Release Alert?

Getting the alert is step one. Acting on it within the right window is what separates teams that use competitive intel from teams that collect it.

Here's the three-step playbook that works for most PMM/founder situations.

---

## Step 1: Verify the Change (5 minutes)

Before forwarding anything to sales or updating a battlecard, confirm:

1. **Open the diff in KompWatch** — click the change in your digest to see the before/after snapshot. Confirm it's what the AI summary says it is (KompWatch is accurate ~95% of the time, but severe misclassifications happen on heavily dynamic pages).

2. **Confirm scope** — is this a publicly launched feature, or a soft test? If the change appeared on a `/beta` or `/labs` path, or if the feature is marked "coming soon" / "early access," calibrate urgency accordingly.

3. **Check if it's already in your battlecard** — sometimes a competitor makes a positioning change that reframes an existing feature. If it's already in your battlecard, you just need to update the framing, not add a new row.

---

## Step 2: Brief Sales Immediately (if a live deal is affected)

This is the most time-sensitive action. If you have deals where this competitor appears:

**Same-day brief (use this template):**
> "FYI — [Competitor] just shipped [feature] (launched [date], confirmed in KompWatch snapshot). If you're in a deal where this comes up, our position is: [1-2 sentence differentiated response]. Battlecard updated."

Send this directly to the AEs in those deals. Don't wait for the weekly PMM sync — the prospect may already know about it.

**When to brief proactively vs. reactively:**
| Severity | Action |
|---|---|
| HIGH (direct competitor, core feature) | Proactive same-day brief to all AEs + Slack in #competitive-intel |
| MEDIUM (adjacent feature, positioning shift) | Add to next weekly competitive brief |
| LOW (minor copy change, no feature signal) | Log in battlecard, no immediate brief needed |

---

## Step 3: Update the Battlecard (within 24 hours)

A high-severity competitor launch that doesn't make it into the battlecard within a day will cause confusion — sales reps will hear about it from prospects before seeing the updated internal response.

**What to update:**
- Add the new feature to the competitor's feature comparison table
- Update your differentiation response if the feature narrows a gap
- Add a "How to handle this objection" entry if the feature is likely to come up in demos
- Update the "Last updated" timestamp on the battlecard

**If you don't have a battlecard yet:** A feature-release alert is a good forcing function to create one. Start with a two-column doc: "Competitor feature" | "Our response." You can expand it later.

---

## Optional: Monitor G2/Capterra Reviews as a Follow-Up Signal

New features generate customer reviews within 2–4 weeks of launch. If a competitor ships something significant, set a 2-week reminder to check their G2/Capterra profile — the reviews will tell you how the feature is landing with real users, which is often more useful for competitive positioning than the launch announcement itself.

KompWatch can monitor a competitor's G2 or Capterra listing directly (add the URL as a competitor slot) to catch new review activity automatically.

---

## Recap: The Three-Step Playbook

1. **Verify** — confirm the change is real and scoped correctly (5 min)
2. **Brief sales** — same-day if any live deals are affected (10 min)
3. **Update battlecard** — within 24 hours for HIGH, weekly batch for MEDIUM/LOW

The goal is to close the gap between "competitor shipped something" and "your sales team has a confident response" to under 24 hours. Most teams that do this manually close that gap in weeks, if ever.
