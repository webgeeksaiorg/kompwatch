# What Is Kompyte's Current Pricing? ($900/Year Entry — No Free Tier)

As of mid-2026, Kompyte's confirmed entry-level price is **$900/year** — with no free tier, no monthly billing option, and no SMB-safe tier. This article explains what that means if you're evaluating Kompyte and whether there are alternatives.

---

## What Is Kompyte's 2026 Pricing?

Kompyte (now under Adobe via the Semrush acquisition) has a confirmed entry price of **$900/year** (~$75/month equivalent). This is the minimum annual commitment to access the platform. Key details:

- **No free tier** — unlike KompWatch, Kompyte offers no permanently free plan. There is no "try with 2 competitors for free" option.
- **No monthly billing** — you must commit annually. Mid-year cancellation may result in forfeiture of remaining prepaid months depending on contract terms.
- **No SMB-safe tier** — the $900/year entry is positioned for teams that can justify an annual CI spend. It is not a hobbyist or early-stage startup option.
- **Sales-gated for higher tiers** — the $900/year tier has competitor limits. Scaling up requires a sales conversation and a new annual contract negotiation.

> **Note:** Pricing above the entry tier is not publicly listed and requires a Semrush/Adobe enterprise sales engagement.

---

## Why Does This Matter?

For early-stage startups, solo founders, and small SaaS teams, $900/year is a non-trivial commitment — especially when:

1. **You can't trial it first** — no free tier means you're committing $900 before you know whether the tool fits your workflow.
2. **You're locked in for a year** — if your team doesn't adopt it or the alerts don't match your use case, you've paid for 12 months.
3. **Competitor limits may force an upgrade** — the entry tier has a cap on tracked competitors. If you monitor more than a handful of domains, you'll hit the ceiling within months and need a new sales negotiation.

This is the pattern that led many small teams to switch from Kompyte to KompWatch after the Adobe/Semrush consolidation.

Consider Kompyte at $900/year if:

- You have 5+ competitors to track and a predictable annual CI budget
- You need a tool your enterprise procurement team has already approved
