# How to Check for Competitor Changes Before a Sales Call

You have a demo or discovery call in 10 minutes. The prospect mentioned they're evaluating your top competitor. Here's how to get a useful competitive snapshot in under 2 minutes using KompWatch.

---

## The 2-Minute Pre-Call Ritual

### Step 1: Open the competitor's page (30 seconds)

Go to [/competitors](https://kompwatch.com/competitors) in KompWatch. Click the competitor you're about to go up against.

You'll see their **change timeline** — a reverse-chronological list of every change KompWatch has detected on their pages.

### Step 2: Look at the last 7 days (45 seconds)

Filter to **last 7 days** and **MEDIUM or higher severity**. You're looking for three things:

| What you see | What it means for your call |
|---|---|
| Pricing page updated | They may have changed tiers, added a new plan, or adjusted price points. Know this before the prospect does. |
| Feature or product page updated | New capability they might demo. Check if it closes a gap they previously had vs. you. |
| Homepage headline changed | Positioning shift — they may be targeting a new ICP or leaning on different proof points. |
| Job listing pages changed | Signals where they're investing. Engineering hires = product roadmap. Sales hires = expansion mode. |
| No changes in 7+ days | Useful signal too — they may be in a quiet period or resource-constrained. |

### Step 3: Note one thing that matters (30 seconds)

You don't need to read everything. Find **one recent change** above MEDIUM severity and ask yourself: *does this affect what the prospect is likely to bring up?*

If yes — write one sentence in your call notes: "Competitor X updated their pricing page on [date] — check if prospect has seen new pricing."

That's it. Your call is now informed.

---

## What If There Are No Recent Changes?

If KompWatch shows no changes in the last 7 days for a competitor, that itself is useful:

- Their pricing and features are stable — don't expect a last-minute surprise
- Mention to the prospect that you track competitor changes in real time — it signals rigor
- Use the absence of change as confidence: your current competitive intel is likely accurate

## After the Call

If the prospect mentioned something about the competitor that KompWatch hadn't flagged yet, use that as a training signal:

1. Go to that competitor's page in KompWatch
2. Check the snapshot history for the relevant section
3. If a change was detected but scored low confidence, click **Mark as Significant** — this helps the model prioritize that type of change in the future

Over time, this makes your pre-call ritual faster and more accurate.
