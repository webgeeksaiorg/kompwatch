# My Competitor Just Raised a Funding Round — What Should I Do in KompWatch?

You heard the news — a competitor closed a Series A, Series B, or announced a strategic investment. Whether you saw it on TechCrunch, LinkedIn, or in a prospect email, this is a high-signal competitive event. Here's what to do in KompWatch right now.

**Short answer:** Adjust your monitoring coverage to the pages most likely to change in the next 30–90 days after a funding event, and set alerts so you're first to know when they act on the money.

---

## Why Funding Rounds Change What You Should Monitor

A competitor with fresh capital typically does a few predictable things within 30–90 days:

1. **Pricing shift** — raise prices (they're now "enterprise-grade") or add a new enterprise tier
2. **Feature expansion** — announce product investment, new capabilities, or integrations
3. **Positioning pivot** — update homepage messaging, ICP language, and value proposition
4. **Hiring surge** — careers page fills up with new engineering, sales, and marketing roles
5. **Marketing push** — new blog content, case studies, compare pages targeting your positioning

Each of these leaves a clear signal on their public website. KompWatch catches all of them automatically — but only if you're watching the right pages.

---

## Immediate Actions in KompWatch (Do These Now)

### 1. Add or verify these six URLs for the funded competitor

If you're already tracking them, check that you have these pages covered. If not, add them immediately:

| Page | Why it matters post-funding |
|------|-----------------------------|
| `/pricing` | First place a pricing or tier change appears |
| `/features` or `/product` | Where new capabilities get announced |
| `/` (homepage) | Positioning and messaging pivots happen here |
| `/careers` or `/jobs` | Hiring surge = where they're investing the money |
| `/blog` | First major announcements often go here |
| `/newsroom` or `/press` | Official announcements, partnership deals |

> **On the free plan**, you're limited to 3 URLs. Prioritize: `/pricing`, `/` (homepage), and `/careers`.

### 2. Set severity threshold to High for this competitor

In **Settings → Notifications**, or on the competitor's detail page, temporarily lower your alert threshold for this specific competitor to **Medium and above**. Post-funding, medium-severity changes (new blog posts, minor copy updates) become more meaningful than usual — they're signals of their GTM motion.

See [Per-Competitor Notification Settings](./per-competitor-notification-settings.md).

### 3. Trigger a manual snapshot now

Before any changes happen, capture a baseline snapshot of their current state. In the competitor detail page, click **"Refresh now"** (or use the [Manual Snapshot Trigger](./manual-snapshot-trigger.md)). This gives you a clean diff reference point before their next update.

### 4. Set a calendar reminder to review in 30 days

Funding-driven changes often take 4–8 weeks to appear on the public site. Set a reminder to do a manual review of their homepage, pricing, and features pages 30 days from today — even if KompWatch hasn't sent you anything. Use that as a check that your monitoring is working.

## What to Watch For (Decoded by Signal Type)

### Pricing page changes
Post-funding pricing moves usually go one of two directions:

- **Upmarket push**: Free tier removed, prices increase, "Enterprise" tier added, "Contact us" replaces a listed price. Means they're targeting larger customers — good or bad depending on your ICP.
- **Land-and-expand**: New low-cost or free tier added. Means they're trying to grow market share aggressively. Watch for feature paywalling.

### Homepage messaging changes
Look for ICP signal words shifting: "growing teams" → "enterprise teams", or "simple tool" → "platform". These pivots usually show up on the homepage before anywhere else.

### Careers page surge
A jump from 5 to 25+ job listings within 2–4 weeks of a funding announcement tells you:
- Which departments get the capital (engineering = product investment, sales = GTM expansion)
- Geographic expansion (new regional roles)
- Potential acquisitions they're building toward (AI/ML engineers hired post-Series B often signal a product bet)

For deeper signal reading, see [Reading Competitor Job Listing Signals](./reading-competitor-job-listing-signals.md).

### Blog / newsroom posts
New posts in the weeks after a funding round often frame the company's "new direction" narrative — these shape the story your prospects will hear. A new case study, a "future of X" thought leadership post, or a feature launch post all indicate where they're positioning themselves next.

## Sharing the Intelligence With Your Team

When KompWatch alerts you to a post-funding change, share it fast:

1. **Sales**: Forward the digest entry to your `#competitive-intel` Slack channel with a note — "Competitor X just launched enterprise tier, watch for this in deals"
2. **Product**: Flag any new features or capabilities to the product team so they can assess the gap
3. **Marketing**: Share messaging changes so they can update battlecards or response content

See [How to Brief Sales on a Competitor Change](./how-to-brief-sales-on-competitor-change.md).

## What If I Wasn't Monitoring This Competitor Yet?

If the funding announcement caught you by surprise and you weren't tracking them at all:

1. Add them to KompWatch immediately (see [Adding Competitors](./adding-competitors.md))
2. You won't have historical data, but you'll start capturing all changes from today
3. For context on what their site looked like before — use the [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org) to pull historical snapshots of their pricing and homepage, giving you a manual baseline
