# Can I Auto-Populate Battlecards from KompWatch Data?

**Not yet** — fully automated battlecard generation (PDF/HTML export) is on the product roadmap. Today, KompWatch handles the **monitoring and signal layer**: it automatically detects competitor changes and gives you AI-summarized, sales-ready intel. You bring the battlecard template and paste the summaries in.

---

## What's Available Today

KompWatch gives you everything you need to keep battlecards fresh — you just assemble the pieces manually:

| KompWatch output | What it maps to on your battlecard |
|---|---|
| AI digest summary of a pricing page change | Pricing section update (paste directly) |
| Feature page change with "What this means for you" note | Features / strengths / weaknesses section |
| Homepage hero copy change | Positioning / messaging section |
| Job listing spike for engineering roles | Forward-looking signal for upcoming features |
| High-severity change flagged in digest | Trigger to schedule a battlecard review |

The AI-generated summaries in your digest are written in plain English, in competitive context — they're designed to paste directly into a battlecard doc with minimal editing.

---

## The Recommended Workflow (Today)

1. **Set up tracking** — add your competitor URLs in KompWatch, optionally add a CSS selector to focus on key sections (pricing grid, feature list, hero copy)
2. **Wait for your digest** — daily on Pro/Team, weekly on Free
3. **Review High + Critical changes** — these are your battlecard-update triggers
4. **Paste the AI summary** into the relevant section of your battlecard (Notion, Google Docs, Guru, Highspot, Seismic, Confluence — wherever you keep them)
5. **Notify your team** — Pro/Team plans include Slack digests so #competitive sees the update automatically

Most teams find the weekly review-and-paste takes 10–20 minutes per competitor — versus hours of manual site-checking without KompWatch.
