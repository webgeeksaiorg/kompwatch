# What's a Realistic KompWatch Setup for a Solo Content Marketer on a $49/mo Budget?

**Short answer:** Pro plan ($49/mo), 10 URL slots, allocated across 2–3 competitors with a mix of blog index pages and individual pillar posts. Weekly review takes ~15 minutes. Total time cost: ~1 hour/month. Here's the exact setup.

---

## The Recommended 10-URL Slot Allocation

### Competitor selection (pick 2–3)

Choose competitors based on **content overlap** — the ones ranking for keywords you also target, or whose blog content directly competes with yours in search. You don't need to monitor every competitor, just the ones who would take organic traffic from you if they outrank you.

Use Ahrefs or Semrush's free tier (or trial) to confirm which 2–3 competitors share the most keyword overlap with your site.

### URL allocation template

| Slot # | URL | What it catches |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `competitor-a.com/blog` | New posts, category changes, blog-level CTA updates |
| 2 | `competitor-a.com/blog/[their-pillar-post-1]` | Content refreshes on their #1 keyword post |
| 3 | `competitor-a.com/blog/[their-pillar-post-2]` | Content refreshes on their #2 keyword post |
| 4 | `competitor-a.com/blog/[their-pillar-post-3]` | Content refreshes on a post competing with yours |
| 5 | `competitor-b.com/blog` | New posts, index-level changes |
| 6 | `competitor-b.com/blog/[their-pillar-post-1]` | Content refreshes on their top post |
| 7 | `competitor-b.com/blog/[their-pillar-post-2]` | Content refreshes on an overlapping keyword post |
| 8 | `competitor-c.com/blog` | New posts only (3rd competitor, lighter coverage) |
| 9 | `competitor-c.com/blog/[their-pillar-post-1]` | Their most strategic content piece |
| 10 | **Flex slot** | Rotate: a competitor resources hub, newsletter archive, or a specific page that's currently hottest |

**If you only have 2 priority competitors**, shift the saved slots to deeper pillar-post coverage on those two (5 posts each instead of 3/2/1).

## How to Identify Which Pillar Posts to Track

### Method 1: Keyword overlap (recommended)

1. Open Ahrefs, Semrush, or Similarweb (free tier is fine to start)
2. Enter a competitor's domain
3. Filter to posts ranking in positions 1–20 for keywords you also have content on
4. The top 3–5 posts by traffic = your monitoring targets

These are the posts most likely to compete with yours in search, and where a content refresh would directly threaten your rankings.

### Method 2: Manual scan

Go to the competitor's blog, filter by "most popular" or "featured" if they offer sorting, and look for:
- Posts with year qualifiers in the title ("Best X in 2026", "Complete guide to Y 2026")
- Long-form guides and comparison posts (high-value, high-maintenance)
- Any post directly covering a use case your product also serves

### Method 3: Start with the blog index alone

If you're not sure which posts matter yet, start with just the 3 blog index pages. After 30 days you'll have a log of every new post each competitor published. From there, you can identify which posts got immediately prominent placement (internal linking, featured sections) — those are their priority pieces.

## The Monday Morning Workflow (15 minutes)

**Monday ~9 AM:** Open your KompWatch digest email (schedule it for Sunday night delivery so it's waiting for you Monday morning).

**Scan HIGH-severity changes (5 min):**
- New content category or topic cluster launched? → add to sprint planning as "counter-content" candidate
- Major pillar post rewrite on a keyword you rank for? → check your own equivalent post's ranking; decide if counter-refresh is needed
- Blog CTA or lead-gen offer changed? → note for messaging awareness, not usually a content action

**Scan MEDIUM-severity changes (5 min):**
- Batch of new posts in one topic cluster? → pattern signal, not urgent; log it
- Single pillar post refresh? → check your equivalent post, decide if it needs a refresh too
- New subdomain or resource hub launched? → evaluate whether to add as a monitoring URL

**Decide + log (5 min):**
- Add any content action items to your sprint board (Notion, Linear, or wherever you plan)
- Dismiss changes that aren't actionable in the next 30 days
- Update your "competitor content tracker" note (a simple shared doc) if you're on a team

**Total time: 15 minutes per week.** Monthly you'll spend ~1 additional hour reviewing the 30-day log to spot multi-week patterns (like a coordinated content-refresh campaign).

## What This Setup Does NOT Cover

Pro at $49/mo is optimized for **blog + content intelligence**. It doesn't include:

- **Slack/Teams notifications** — those require Team plan ($149/mo). On Pro, all alerts come via email digest.
- **6-hourly monitoring** — Pro snapshots daily. For most content strategy use cases, daily is more than sufficient (content refreshes don't require real-time detection). Team unlocks 6-hourly if you want faster signals.
- **API access** — for piping changes into a custom workflow or CMS, Team plan or Enterprise.
- **Competitor ad monitoring** — KompWatch doesn't track paid ads; that's a separate tool category (SpyFu, AdBeat).

If you eventually need Slack routing or hourly checks, the upgrade path to Team is straightforward and monthly billing means no long-term commitment.

## Getting Started Checklist

- [ ] Sign up for Pro plan at [kompwatch.com/pricing](/pricing)
- [ ] Add your top 2–3 competitors (name + primary domain)
- [ ] Add 3 blog index URLs (one per competitor)
- [ ] Use Ahrefs/Semrush trial to identify 2–3 pillar posts per competitor that overlap your keywords
- [ ] Add those pillar posts as additional URL slots (fill your 10 slots)
- [ ] Set digest frequency: **Daily** for priority competitors, **Weekly** for lower-priority ones
- [ ] Schedule digest delivery for **Sunday 8 PM** → lands in inbox for Monday morning review

First month: mostly learning what "normal" looks like for each competitor. Month 2–3 is when you start seeing the patterns worth acting on.
