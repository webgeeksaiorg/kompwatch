# How Do I Brief My Sales Team on a Competitor Change in Under 5 Minutes?

When KompWatch detects a meaningful competitor change — a pricing cut, a new feature launch, a repositioned homepage — your sales team needs to know before their next call. This guide shows you how to turn a KompWatch alert into a 30-second sales brief in under 5 minutes.

---

## Why Speed Matters More Than Depth

Sales reps don't need a 10-page competitive analysis. They need one thing: *what changed, and what do I say if a prospect brings it up?*

The faster you get that in front of them, the more prepared they are. A KompWatch digest email turns into a usable brief in minutes — no BI team, no Slack thread, no waiting.

---

## The 5-Minute Brief Workflow

### Step 1 — Identify the change (30 seconds)

Open the KompWatch alert email or go to [kompwatch.com/digests](https://kompwatch.com/digests). Scan the change summary:

- **What changed?** (Pricing page / Feature list / Homepage / Changelog)
- **Severity?** HIGH = brief the team now. MEDIUM = add to weekly update. LOW = archive.
- **AI confidence badge?** Green (≥ 80%) = confirmed real change. Amber = verify before briefing.

If the change is HIGH severity + green badge: proceed immediately to step 2.

### Step 2 — Visit the competitor page directly (60 seconds)

Click the competitor URL in the alert. Confirm the change is live. You want to see it with your own eyes before quoting it to a sales rep.

Look for:
- **New pricing**: What tier was added, removed, or repriced? Is there a new trial offer or discount?
- **New feature claim**: What's the headline? Is it something your product already does?
- **Repositioned messaging**: Did they move upmarket ("enterprise-grade") or downmarket ("free for small teams")?

### Step 3 — Write a one-paragraph brief (2 minutes)

This doesn't need to be polished. Format:

> **[Competitor name] changed their pricing page as of [today's date].**
>
> What changed: [one sentence — "Dropped their starter plan from $99 to $59/mo."]
>
> What it means for us: [one sentence — "They're now cheaper in the SMB band. If a prospect brings it up, highlight our [differentiator] which they still don't offer."]
>
> What to say if asked: [one sentence — "Their price dropped but so did their feature scope — the new $59 tier removes [X]."]

That's your brief. It fits in a Slack message.

### Step 4 — Share it (60 seconds)

Three options:

**Option A — Slack (fastest).** Paste the brief into your `#competitive` or `#sales-enablement` channel. Tag your AEs or SDR lead. Include the KompWatch change link for anyone who wants the full diff.

**Option B — Email forward.** If your team uses email: forward the KompWatch digest email with your one-paragraph brief pasted at the top. Subject line: `[COMPETITOR] pricing change — read before your next call`.

**Option C — Shared report link.** From the change detail page, click **Share** to generate a shareable link (no KompWatch login required). Paste that into your CRM deal record, or send to an AE who needs context for a specific deal.

---

## When to Brief vs. When to Archive

Not every change needs a brief. Use this filter:

| Change type | Severity | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor cut pricing by 20%+ | HIGH | Brief immediately |
| Competitor launched new plan targeting your ICP | HIGH | Brief immediately |
| Competitor added a feature you don't have | HIGH | Brief + flag for product |
| Competitor changed homepage headline/positioning | MEDIUM | Weekly competitive digest |
| Minor copy change, blog post, social proof update | LOW | Archive — no action needed |
| A/B test or CDN flicker (Amber AI badge) | Any | Verify first, then decide |

## Setting Up Automatic Briefs for Your Team

If you want this process to run without manual effort:

1. **Connect Slack** — go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and add a webhook to your `#competitive` channel. HIGH-severity changes automatically post a summary to Slack as soon as they're detected.
2. **Set digest frequency to Daily** — your team gets a morning briefing of everything detected in the last 24 hours, ready before standup.
3. **Configure minimum severity** — set instant alerts to HIGH only, so the Slack channel doesn't become noisy. MEDIUM and LOW still appear in the digest.

After this setup, KompWatch handles the "detect and notify" layer automatically. Your only job is the 60-second manual step of writing a sentence of context before forwarding.

---

## The Pre-Call Ritual (For Active Competitive Deals)

For deals where you know a competitor is involved, run this 2-minute check before every call:

1. Open KompWatch → go to the competitor's page
2. Sort **Change History** by date — check for anything in the last 7 days
3. If there's a new HIGH change: note it in your call prep doc
4. If there's nothing new: proceed normally (no change is also intel — stable product, no discounting)

This takes 2 minutes and means you're never surprised by a change the prospect saw before you did.
