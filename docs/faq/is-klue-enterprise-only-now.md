# Is Klue Enterprise-Only Now?

**Short answer:** Klue has always required an enterprise sales process, but as of 2026 their pricing and positioning has moved further upmarket — making Klue functionally unavailable to teams with budgets under $20K/year. If you're a small or mid-market team evaluating Klue, here's what you need to know.

---

## What Is Klue's Current Pricing and Target Market?

Klue does not publicly list pricing. Their contract entry point is typically **$20,000–$30,000/year**, requiring a sales call, a negotiation process, and a minimum 12-month commitment. The platform is marketed to revenue teams at mid-to-large B2B companies — particularly teams with dedicated competitive intelligence analysts and established battlecard workflows.

In 2025–2026, Klue's product development has accelerated upmarket:

- **Klue Compete Agent** (March 2026) — AI-powered competitive intel inside Microsoft Teams, Dynamics 365, and Copilot. Requires a Microsoft enterprise stack.
- **Klue AI Win-Loss Suite** — Revenue-team AI for analyzing won/lost deal patterns. Priced for organizations with significant deal volume and CRM history.
- **Klue Salesforce integration** — Deep native CRM embedding for Salesforce enterprise accounts.

Each of these moves signals the same direction: Klue is optimizing for large enterprise revenue teams, not startups or small product teams.

---

## Why This Matters for SMB and Mid-Market Teams

Klue's value proposition assumes you have:
1. A dedicated competitive intelligence analyst (or program manager) to curate the platform
2. A sales team large enough to benefit from battlecard distribution at scale
3. A Microsoft or Salesforce enterprise stack
4. A $20K+/year CI budget

Without these conditions, you're paying enterprise prices for a platform designed for enterprise workflows — and you'll likely experience the same "shelfware" outcome that the CI market has documented repeatedly. See [Why CI Tools Become Shelfware →](./why-ci-tools-become-shelfware.md)
