# KompWatch for Klue Users

If you're currently on Klue and evaluating lighter-weight alternatives — whether at renewal, after a team restructure, or after Klue's 2025–2026 layoff rounds — this page explains how KompWatch compares and whether switching makes sense for your team.

## I Already Have Klue — Why Would I Pay for KompWatch?

Klue is a full competitive intelligence platform with a median contract of $50K/yr (Vendr, 106 deals). It covers battlecard generation, win/loss workflows, a Compete Agent AI layer, and a Microsoft Copilot MCP Server — but that scope comes at a price most self-serve SaaS teams can't justify.

Two Klue layoff rounds in under 18 months (2025 and May 2026, cutting ~40% then ~30% of staff) have shaken customer confidence. Their CEO acknowledged losing deals to ChatGPT — a signal that a large share of Klue's customer value was in the "summarize what a competitor does" layer, which LLMs now provide for free. The remaining bet is on Microsoft Teams/Dynamics 365 integration. If your team isn't deeply embedded in the Microsoft enterprise stack, you may be paying for a platform that's pivoting away from your workflow.

KompWatch does one thing: monitors competitor websites and explains what changed — in plain English, automatically, no analyst required.

## What Happens to My Klue Data?

KompWatch cannot import competitor lists or change history from Klue. Your monitoring history starts fresh when you add competitors.

Before switching, export any historical battlecard content, win/loss data, or change summaries from Klue — especially if you've built internal reports from them.

## How Do I Switch?

1. **Sign up at [kompwatch.com](https://kompwatch.com)** — free, no credit card required on the Free plan.
2. **Add the same competitor URLs** you tracked in Klue. See [Adding a Competitor](./adding-competitors.md).
3. **Set CSS selectors** to target specific sections — pricing table, feature comparison, changelog. Optional but improves signal quality significantly. See [CSS Selector Targeting](./css-selectors.md).
4. **Connect Slack** in Settings → Integrations if you use a `#competitive` channel.

Your first snapshot runs immediately. Change detection starts after the second snapshot — typically within 24 hours on Free or a few hours on Pro.

## Can I Try KompWatch Without Canceling Klue?

Yes. KompWatch's free tier (2 competitors, no credit card) lets you run both tools in parallel. Add your two most important competitors to KompWatch and compare digest quality against what Klue surfaces during your trial period.
