# What Makes Free Users Upgrade to Pro?

If you're on the free tier and wondering whether it's worth upgrading — or if you're already a paid user trying to understand what triggered your decision — this page explains the activation moments that typically push users from free to Pro.

## The Five Moments That Typically Trigger Upgrades

### 1. The Missed Change

The most common trigger: a user discovers — from a customer, a sales call, a tweet — that a competitor changed their pricing two weeks ago, and KompWatch caught it, but only after the daily snapshot. They needed to know *the day it happened*, not the next morning.

If you're tracking competitors whose pricing or feature moves are tactically important, daily snapshots are too slow. 6-hour snapshots on Pro mean you see it the same day.

### 2. The Third Competitor Problem

Free users who've added their two allowed competitors almost always have a third they want to add. The moment you hit the limit and realize you're leaving a competitor unmonitored, the $49 math is easy.

### 3. The Team Share Moment

Someone on your team asks for the CI update. You realize you want to forward the digest, share a change, or set up a Slack alert — and none of that works cleanly on free. Pro gives you automatic digest delivery, team sharing, and webhook integration.

### 4. The First High-Signal Alert

When KompWatch detects a genuinely important change — a competitor dropping their most popular plan tier, launching a new free offering that competes directly with your paid tier — the ROI of $49/mo becomes immediately obvious. One good catch pays for 2+ years.

### 5. The "It's Actually Working" Realization

After two or three weeks of seeing accurate, non-noisy change alerts on the free plan, some users upgrade not because of a specific event, but because they realize the tool is reliable. They want more coverage (more competitors, more frequency) now that they trust the signal quality.

## Is the Jump from Free to Pro Worth It?

Honest answer: it depends on how many competitors you're tracking and how tactically important those competitors are.

**Upgrade makes sense if:**
- You have more than 2 competitors worth watching
- You need to know about pricing or feature changes within hours, not days
- You want to share CI with a sales or product team
- You're actively in competitive deals where knowing what a competitor just changed matters

**Stay free if:**
- You're in early evaluation and not sure which competitors to track yet
- Your competitive landscape moves slowly (weekly/monthly changes are fine)
- You're solo and don't need team-sharing features
