# What Is Klue's Minimum Contract for Small Teams?

**Short answer:** Klue does not offer a self-serve plan. Their confirmed minimum entry contract is approximately **$1,500/month ($18,000/year)**, requires a sales engagement, and is designed for mid-to-large enterprise revenue teams — not small or early-stage teams. If you're a small team that hit Klue's pricing wall, here's what you need to know.

---

## What Is Klue's Actual Minimum Price?

Klue does not publish pricing. Based on confirmed signals from Vendr data, G2 reviews, and procurement reports as of 2026:

- **Minimum entry contract: ~$1,500/month (~$18,000/year)**
- Annual commitment required — no monthly billing option
- Multi-seat contracts start at this floor; per-seat costs scale up from there
- Onboarding services are often required as an additional line item

This puts Klue firmly in enterprise contract territory. A team of 3–5 people paying $18,000/year for CI tooling is paying **$3,600–$6,000 per person per year** for competitive intelligence infrastructure.

---

## Why Small Teams Run Into the Klue Pricing Wall

Klue's product is built for companies that have:

1. **A dedicated CI analyst or competitive intelligence program manager** — Klue is not self-serve in any meaningful sense. Someone needs to curate the battlecards, manage integrations, and keep the intelligence corpus fresh.
2. **A large enough sales team** to justify battlecard distribution at scale (Klue Compete Agent, CRM integrations, Slack channels)
3. **A Microsoft or Salesforce enterprise stack** — Klue Compete Agent (March 2026) is built for Teams, Dynamics 365, and Copilot. Value diminishes significantly outside the Microsoft stack.
4. **$18K+ annual CI budget** as a committed line item

For solo founders, seed-stage startups, Series A companies, and growth-stage teams with lean GTM structures, none of these conditions typically apply. Klue's platform is overbuilt for these use cases and underused as a result.

---

## What Does $1,500/Month Actually Get You in Klue?

At the entry tier (~$18K/year), you typically get:
- A set number of tracked competitor domains (exact cap varies by contract)
- Access to Klue's AI battlecard generation
- Slack integration for alert delivery
- Standard onboarding package

What you do **not** get at entry tier:
- Klue Compete Agent (Teams/Dynamics/Copilot integration — higher tier)
- Klue Win-Loss AI suite (additional tier)
- Priority support or dedicated CSM (enterprise tier)

In other words, the $1,500/month entry buys you the core platform. The highest-value AI features (Compete Agent, Win-Loss) are behind additional paywalls.

## Is Klue Worth $18,000/Year for a Small Team?

Only if all of the following are true:
- You have a dedicated competitive intelligence person to manage the platform
- You have a sales team of 15+ reps who would actively use battlecards
- You're operating on a Microsoft stack (Teams, Dynamics 365, Copilot)
- Your CI budget is a committed annual line item

If you're a PM, founder, or small GTM team member who just wants to **know when a competitor changes their pricing page or launches a new feature**, Klue is not the right tool at any price. You'd pay enterprise rates for infrastructure designed around enterprise workflows.
