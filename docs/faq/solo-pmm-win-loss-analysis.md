# Do I Need a Full CI Team to Run Win/Loss Analysis, or Can a Solo PMM Do It?

**Short answer:** Solo PMM (or founder, or head of marketing) is the primary user profile for KompWatch. The monitoring, classification, and summarization work is handled by the tool — you're consuming a digest, not running an analyst program.

---

## What a solo PMM win/loss workflow looks like

Three time investments per week/month:

| Task | Time | When |
|---|---|---|
| Read your KompWatch digest | 15 min/week | Every Monday |
| Review competitor change history for a Closed-Lost deal | 5 min/deal | At deal close |
| Update battlecards from HIGH-severity changes | 30 min/month | Monthly review |

That's it. KompWatch handles the mechanical work of checking competitor pages, diffing them, classifying what changed, and surfacing only what's significant. You handle the interpretation.

---

## What you don't need

- A dedicated CI analyst
- A full-time program manager
- Buyer interview infrastructure
- A Salesforce admin to set up integrations
- A $30–60K/yr win/loss suite

---

## What a team with a CI analyst looks like (for comparison)

If you do have a CI analyst, KompWatch becomes their monitoring layer rather than the whole program. The analyst spends time on:

- **Strategic synthesis** — connecting competitor patterns to product roadmap decisions
- **Buyer interview facilitation** — sourcing and running post-decision calls
- **Competitive strategy docs** — full competitive positioning documents, not just battlecards
- **Win/loss facilitation** — structured deal reviews with sales leadership

The tool replaces the mechanical page-checking that would otherwise eat 5–10 hours a week.

---

## Starting a win/loss program with no analyst budget

Three moves to get a lightweight program running in one afternoon:

1. **Add your top 3–5 competitors to KompWatch** — pick the ones that show up most on Closed-Lost deals in your CRM. Add each competitor's `/pricing`, `/product`, and homepage.

2. **For every deal that closes this quarter**, spend 5 minutes reviewing the competitor's KompWatch change history for the 30–60 days before close. Look for pricing drops, new feature announcements, or messaging changes that could have influenced the evaluation.

3. **Add a single freeform field to your CRM deal record** — "competitor change context" — and paste the top 1–3 relevant KompWatch entries. After 60–90 days you'll have a searchable dataset of competitor moves correlated with your deal outcomes.

**Total investment:** Zero analyst hours, $49/mo (Pro plan).

---

## When to level up

After 60–90 days of running a lightweight KompWatch program, you'll know whether to invest more:

- If you see clear patterns linking competitor changes to deal outcomes → add a buyer-interview layer (Clozd, Wynter) to get the buyer's side of the story
- If competitive losses are a top-5 business problem → consider a full win/loss suite (Klue, Crayon)
- If you're still not sure what's driving losses → stay with KompWatch and add a "lost to" field to your CRM if you haven't already
