# Does KompWatch Replace Semrush .Trends (Traffic Analytics)?

**Short answer:** No — and that's intentional. KompWatch replaces the Kompyte / competitor-monitoring workflow inside Semrush, not the .Trends traffic-intelligence and market-sizing modules. If you need traffic estimates, keyword share, or industry benchmarks, you'll want a dedicated tool for that alongside KompWatch.

---

## What Does Semrush .Trends Do?

Semrush .Trends (previously Traffic Analytics + Market Explorer) gives you:

- **Estimated website traffic** for competitor domains — monthly visits, traffic trends, source breakdown
- **Market share** — how traffic is distributed across competitors in a category
- **Audience overlap** — what percentage of your visitors also visit a competitor's site
- **Industry benchmarks** — top sites by traffic in a given market segment

These are market-intelligence tools, not change-monitoring tools. They answer "how big is this competitor?" and "how are market dynamics shifting?" — not "what changed on this competitor's website last week?"

---

## What Does KompWatch Do Instead?

KompWatch monitors the *content* of competitor websites — not traffic estimates:

| Signal | KompWatch | Semrush .Trends |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor pricing changes | ✓ Detected on next snapshot | ✗ Not available |
| New feature announcements | ✓ Flagged with severity | ✗ Not available |
| Positioning / messaging shifts | ✓ AI summary of what changed | ✗ Not available |
| Hiring signals (job page changes) | ✓ Monitored automatically | ✗ Not available |
| Estimated monthly traffic | ✗ Not available | ✓ Core feature |
| Traffic source breakdown | ✗ Not available | ✓ Core feature |
| Keyword ranking overlap | ✗ Not available | ✓ via Position Tracking |
| Market share estimates | ✗ Not available | ✓ via Market Explorer |

The tools answer different questions. KompWatch is a **change-detection layer** — it tells you when something on a competitor's website changes and what it means. .Trends is a **traffic-intelligence layer** — it tells you how much attention a competitor is getting.

## Can I Use KompWatch and a .Trends Alternative Together?

Yes — this is the recommended stack for teams who previously relied on Semrush for both workflows:

1. **KompWatch** — for change monitoring: pricing shifts, messaging updates, feature launches, hiring signals ($0–$149/mo)
2. **Ahrefs, Similarweb, or SE Ranking** — for traffic and keyword intelligence

Running both is almost always cheaper than a Semrush Business plan, and you get better-focused tools for each job.

## What If I Only Used .Trends Occasionally?

If you used .Trends a few times a quarter for a snapshot of market sizing or share — not as a core workflow — many teams find they can replace that with:

- **Free tools:** SimilarWeb's free tier, Google Trends, SparkToro (audience intelligence)
- **Manual research:** Crunchbase, LinkedIn headcount data, G2 review velocity as proxy signals for company growth
- **KompWatch job-listing signals:** Growth in hiring across specific roles (engineering, sales, marketing) is often a more actionable growth signal than traffic estimates
