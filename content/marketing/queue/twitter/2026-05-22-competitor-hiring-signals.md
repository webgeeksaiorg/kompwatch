Most product teams check competitor pricing pages.

Nobody checks competitor job postings.

That's a mistake. A competitor that posts 5 ML engineer roles in 6 weeks is shipping an AI feature in 3–9 months. A competitor that stops hiring customer success reps is either scaling or stalling.

Pricing tells you where they are. Hiring tells you where they're going.
