One thing I'd add: knowing *when* a competitor changed their price matters as much as knowing *that* they changed it.

If they dropped price 6 weeks ago and you're finding out in a sales call today, you've been having 6 weeks of conversations without the right context.

The lag is the problem. Knowing in real-time means you can adjust messaging proactively. Finding out from a prospect mid-demo means you're reacting.

That's the core reason I built KompWatch — the information was always out there, I was just getting it too late.
