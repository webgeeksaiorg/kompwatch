Competitor job listings are the most underrated CI signal.

Three ML engineer postings in 6 weeks = they're building something AI-powered. Two "Enterprise Account Executive" roles = they're moving upmarket. "Head of Partnerships" = ecosystem play incoming.

The website copy won't tell you this for another 6 months. The jobs page tells you now.

I watch jobs pages the same way I watch pricing pages. Most of the time nothing. Occasionally, a real signal.
