Your competitor's changelog is the best signal in competitive intelligence.

Nobody reads it.

It announces features before any press release, before any sales enablement deck gets updated, before their reps know to pitch it. It's public, it's timestamped, and it's almost always ignored.

We watch 7 pages per competitor by default. The changelog is always one of them.
