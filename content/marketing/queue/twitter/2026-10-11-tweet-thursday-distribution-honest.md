Distribution is harder than I thought. By a lot.

The product is in a pretty good place. Scraper works, AI digests are genuinely useful, zero data loss in the last 6 weeks.

But getting people to find it? Different problem entirely.

SEO is slow. Cold outreach feels gross when you're the product. Paid ads I haven't figured out yet.

What's working: Reddit (genuine participation, not spam), people who find the blog posts, word of mouth from existing users.

What's not: everything else tbh.

Building in public is partly therapeutic. You get to say "I don't know what I'm doing yet" and someone always replies "same."
