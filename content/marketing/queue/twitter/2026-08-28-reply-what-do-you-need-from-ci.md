Depends what you're actually trying to know.

If it's "did their pricing change" or "did they ship a new feature" — you don't need Crayon. You need something that watches 3-4 pages and emails you.

If it's deep win-loss analysis + full sales enablement — then yeah, Crayon or Klue, but budget $20K+ and a dedicated CI person.

Most startups I talk to need the first thing and buy nothing because the second thing is too expensive.
