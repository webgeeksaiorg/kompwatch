The thing Crayon won't tell you: most teams don't need competitive intelligence software. They need competitive *awareness*.

Software is fine. But if the PM doesn't actually read the weekly digest, you've spent $500/mo on a notification nobody opens.

Start with a stupid simple script that diffs three pages and emails you. See if anyone reads it.
