CI workflow that doesn't require a dedicated analyst:

1. Pick 3-5 competitor pages that actually matter (pricing, features, hero)
2. Get automated alerts when those pages change
3. Review the diff — is this significant or noise?
4. If significant: update your battlecard, ping the AE who's in a deal

That's it. No weekly 3-hour review sessions. No tool nobody opens.
