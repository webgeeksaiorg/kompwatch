Depends what you actually need. Klue/Crayon are full CI platforms — battlecards, sales enablement, Slack integrations — and they price accordingly ($10K+/year minimum).

If all you want is "tell me when a competitor's pricing page changes," that's 5% of what those tools do and you shouldn't pay for the other 95%.

There are lighter tools (including mine, KompWatch — $49/mo) that just do the website monitoring piece. Worth matching the tool to the actual problem before signing an annual contract.
