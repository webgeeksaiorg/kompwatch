The six pages I watch on every competitor, in priority order:

1. /pricing — obvious
2. /changelog or /releases — shows what they're actually shipping
3. /careers — hiring signals, see other thread
4. /features or /product — positioning changes matter
5. Home hero — what problem they claim to solve shifts with market perception
6. /compare/* — who they're targeting tells you who you're fighting for

Start with pricing. Add the rest one at a time. You don't need all of them on day one.
