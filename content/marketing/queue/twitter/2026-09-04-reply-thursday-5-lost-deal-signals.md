The worst lost deal debrief: "we lost because they launched X in March and we didn't know until now."

X was on their roadmap since January. It was on their /product page in February. The job listings for that area started in October.

Three early signals. All of them visible. None of them caught.

That's a monitoring problem, not a product problem.
