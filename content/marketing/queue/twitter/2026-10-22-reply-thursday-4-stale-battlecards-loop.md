The stale battlecard problem is a monitoring problem, not a writing problem.

Most teams update battlecards after a loss review. That's already 60-90 days behind. The competitor changed something, won the deals in that window, and you found out from the loss.

Alert on the change → update the card that day. That's the loop. Takes 5 minutes instead of a quarterly project.
