depends on what "tracking" means for you.

if you want to know when their website content changes → automated diffing (we do this)

if you want social/news mentions → Google Alerts, Mention.com

if you want intent data, review signals, sales battlecards → Crayon/Klue (budget: $15-25K/yr)

most people need the first thing and buy the third. then wonder why nobody uses it.
