Pricing page changes follow a pattern I've noticed across 200+ competitors tracked in KompWatch:

1. "Contact us for pricing" shows up on the page (~4 weeks before a price increase)
2. Free tier gets an asterisk added
3. The old price disappears from the title tag first (most people miss this)

If you see #1, start prepping your retention playbook.
