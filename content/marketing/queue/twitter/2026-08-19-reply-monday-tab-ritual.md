I ran a Monday morning ritual for two years. 12 tabs, each pinned to a competitor page. Open each one, scan for changes, copy anything notable into a Notion doc.

30-45 minutes every single Monday. Things I missed:
— A pricing change that went live on Thursday
— A features page rewrite that happened over a weekend
— A new case study targeting my exact ICP that was up for 3 weeks before I noticed

The cron job I eventually wrote caught more in a week than I caught in months manually.
