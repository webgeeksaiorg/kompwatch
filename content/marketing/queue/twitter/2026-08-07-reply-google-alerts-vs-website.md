Google Alerts catches blog posts and press releases. 

It doesn't catch a pricing page quietly swapping from monthly to annual-only. Or a competitor sunsetting their free tier. Or a "Contact Sales" replacing the signup button.

Those are the changes that matter. They happen on the website, not in a press release.

That's the gap.
