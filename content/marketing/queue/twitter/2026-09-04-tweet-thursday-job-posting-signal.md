A competitor posting for a "Senior ML Engineer – Search" tells you more about their roadmap than their next 3 blog posts.

Job listings are the only place companies accidentally tell the truth about what they're building.

I monitor careers pages for this reason. When one of our tracked competitors posted 4 ML roles in August, they announced an "AI-powered" product line in October.

The gap is about 90 days, consistently.
