the reason battlecards go stale isn't that nobody cares. it's that updating them requires someone to manually notice a competitor changed something, then remember to update the doc, then get it to the sales team.

three handoffs. all manual. any one of them can fail silently.

if you automate the "notice something changed" step, the rest gets much easier.
