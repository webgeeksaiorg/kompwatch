The four pages that actually matter:

1. Pricing (obvious)
2. Changelog/release notes (huge — tells you their roadmap)
3. Jobs page (what they're hiring signals what they're building)
4. /compare pages — they'll literally tell you how they're positioning against you

Everything else is noise. The homepage barely changes.
