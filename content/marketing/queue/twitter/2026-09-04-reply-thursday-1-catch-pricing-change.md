The only reliable method I've found: automated snapshot diff of their pricing page, daily.

Everything else — Google Alerts, RSS, manual checks — misses it. GA doesn't index pricing page copy. Manual checks require you to remember what the old price was.

First time you catch a competitor raise mid-sales-cycle, it pays for itself.
