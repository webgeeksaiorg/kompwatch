Counterintuitive thing I've noticed watching competitor websites:

Silence can be the most important signal.

A competitor that's been updating their pricing page every 3 weeks for a quarter — and then suddenly goes quiet for 6 weeks — is probably preparing something.

Not always. Sometimes they just stopped testing. But the pattern of frequent-then-nothing has preceded a launch announcement twice in the last month for KompWatch users.

You can't catch the silence if you're checking manually. You only notice it when you have the history.
