The problem with static battlecards: they're always describing a competitor that no longer exists.

By the time a battlecard gets written, reviewed, approved, and uploaded to your sales wiki — the competitor already shipped two new features and changed pricing.

Real-time monitoring doesn't replace battlecards. It keeps them from being embarrassing.
