Google Alerts is fine for brand mentions and press coverage.

It's terrible for tracking actual website changes — pricing tweaks, feature page rewrites, positioning pivots. None of that surfaces in Google's index quickly enough to matter, and it won't catch changes on pages that aren't getting new backlinks.

The gap is website-level diff monitoring. That's a scraper problem, not a search problem.
