Three weeks ago I'd have told you the hardest part of building KompWatch was the scraper.

(SPAs are genuinely annoying. Some sites rerender the whole DOM on scroll. We use Playwright and it still breaks on weird edge cases.)

Turns out the hard part is behavior change. People already have a habit. It's called "I'll check their pricing page later." Getting them to stop checking manually and just trust the alert — that's the real friction.

Building a product is easy. Replacing a habit is hard.
