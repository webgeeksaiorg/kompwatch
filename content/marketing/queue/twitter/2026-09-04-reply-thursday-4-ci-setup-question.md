Start with the question "what would change my roadmap priority or my pitch?"

That tells you exactly what to monitor. If a competitor killing their free tier would matter, watch their pricing page. If a competitor launching API access would matter, watch their /features page and job listings.

Don't monitor everything. Monitor the stuff that, if it changed, you'd want to know within 24 hours.
