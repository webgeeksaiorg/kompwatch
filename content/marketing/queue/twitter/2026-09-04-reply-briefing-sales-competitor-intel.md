We tried Slack channels ("competitor-intel"), weekly emails, and Notion docs.

All three died the same death: nobody maintains them when it's added to someone's todo list instead of automated.

What works now: automated weekly digest (KompWatch sends it every Monday) that only includes actual website changes, not noise. Subject line is "[Competitor X] changed 2 things this week — here's what."

Sales reps read it because it's short and it's about specific changes, not analysis. They want facts before a call, not frameworks.

Less than 5 minutes to skim. That's the bar.
