Battlecard rot is a symptom, not the problem.

The problem: your CI workflow produces alerts but no ownership. Alert fires, Slack gets a message, nobody updates the doc.

6 weeks later someone in a demo uses the old battlecard. Competitor's pricing changed in April. Your AE is quoting the wrong comparison.

The fix isn't a better battlecard template. It's making sure someone sees the change and is responsible for the 2-minute update.

Alerts are the easy part.
