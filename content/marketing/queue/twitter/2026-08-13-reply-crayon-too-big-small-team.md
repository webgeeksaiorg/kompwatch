Crayon is incredible if you have $30K+ to spend and a dedicated CI analyst. If you're a 10-person startup it's just... a lot.

The pattern I see: companies buy Crayon, it sits mostly unused because nobody has time to sift through it, renewal comes and they quietly downgrade or cancel.

There's a real gap at $49/mo that just does the watching-and-alerting part without the analyst workflow attached.
