What I'd do on no budget: pick 3 competitors, 3 pages each (pricing, features, /alternatives). Set up free monitoring for those 9 URLs.

Don't monitor the blog. Don't monitor press releases. Just those 9 pages, daily.

That covers 80% of the changes that matter for sales and positioning. The blog is vanity. The pricing page is where the actual moves happen.

If you outgrow free tiers, that's when $49/mo makes sense.
