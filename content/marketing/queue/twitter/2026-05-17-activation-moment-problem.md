Here's what I think is happening with free → paid conversion on KompWatch.

Users sign up, add 3-5 competitors, get their first digest. It's useful. But "useful" isn't the same as "I need to pay for this."

The customers who convert tell me about a specific moment: they caught a competitor pricing change before a prospect brought it up on a sales call. Or they noticed a competitor quietly added a feature they'd been planning — two weeks before their roadmap review.

The tool didn't just give them information. It made them look prepared in a room full of people.

That's the moment. I'm not surfacing it fast enough.
