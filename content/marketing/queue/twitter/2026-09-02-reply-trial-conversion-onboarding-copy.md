our problem was the first action was too vague.

"Add your first competitor" — what does that mean? their domain? a specific page? all their pages?

changed it to "Paste your competitor's pricing page URL."

trial-to-first-alert went from 3.1 days to 18 hours.

one concrete instruction > one abstract instruction every time.
