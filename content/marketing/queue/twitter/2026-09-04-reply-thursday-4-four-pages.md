Four pages. That's it.

1. Pricing (obviously)
2. Features/product page (feature additions/removals)
3. Homepage hero (messaging shifts — leading indicator of positioning change)
4. Jobs/careers (hiring signals)

Everything else is noise. Blog, about, case studies — changes there won't affect your next deal. Those four will.
