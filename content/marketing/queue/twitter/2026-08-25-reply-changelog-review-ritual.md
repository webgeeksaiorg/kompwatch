One workflow that's actually stuck for me: competitor changelog review every other Tuesday, 20 minutes max.

I scan what changed, write one sentence on what I think it signals, file it. At quarterly planning I pull the file. It's usually more useful than anything I got from Gong calls about competitors.

The catch: you have to be monitoring consistently or the changelog review is blind.
