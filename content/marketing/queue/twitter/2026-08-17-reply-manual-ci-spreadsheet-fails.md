[Reply angle: someone describing their manual competitor tracking spreadsheet]

The spreadsheet phase is universal. Every team hits it.

Date column. Competitor column. "Notes" column with two entries from six months ago. Then it stops because updating it is now another job nobody owns.

What breaks the habit isn't tools — it's direction. The sheet makes you go look for changes. You want changes to come find you.

Different mental model entirely once you make that switch.
