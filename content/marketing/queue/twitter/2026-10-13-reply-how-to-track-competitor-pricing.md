Few things that actually work:

1. Watch the /pricing page directly (not just Google Alerts — those catch news, not page edits)
2. Watch the /alternatives or comparison pages — that's where they reveal positioning
3. Watch job listings — hiring 3 ML engineers in infra signals a product direction change
4. Headless browser for SPAs, not RSS — most pricing pages are React and don't ping feeds

That's basically what I built KompWatch to do. Still tweaking the SPA edge cases.
