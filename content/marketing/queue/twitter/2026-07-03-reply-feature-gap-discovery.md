When a customer mentions a competitor feature you didn't know existed, that's two problems at once:

1. Your competitor is shipping faster than your awareness
2. That feature was customer-visible long enough for your customer to notice it and bring it up to you

The gap between "competitor shipped it" and "customer raised it in a call" is usually 3-6 weeks. That's 3-6 weeks of prospects comparing you to something you didn't know was on the table.
