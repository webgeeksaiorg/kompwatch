Annual pricing discounts are the sneakiest competitor move to miss.

They don't change the monthly headline number. They add "save 20% annually" in small text. Or flip the toggle default from monthly to annual so the page *looks* cheaper.

Nobody sets up an alert for "pricing page changed but the big number looks the same."

Meanwhile their effective monthly rate just dropped $40 and you're still pitching your price as cheaper.
