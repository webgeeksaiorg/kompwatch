Most "competitor monitoring" is actually just checking if they announced something on Twitter.

That's not monitoring. That's following.

Monitoring is: did their pricing page change? Did they add a new tier? Did the hero copy shift to a different ICP? Did the /features page quietly drop the thing they were winning deals with?

None of that shows up on Twitter. You have to watch the pages themselves.
