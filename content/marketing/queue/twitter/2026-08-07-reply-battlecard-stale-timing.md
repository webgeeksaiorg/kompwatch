The problem isn't that battlecards go stale. It's that you don't know when they go stale.

Your competitor changes their positioning on Tuesday. You find out 3 months later when a sales rep uses the wrong objection handle and loses the deal.

Automated monitoring doesn't fix everything. But at least you know something changed within 24 hours.

That's the gap between winning and guessing.
