The issue is usually the gap between detection and update — not the format of the battlecard itself.

Most teams find out about a competitor change through a customer comment or a lost deal. By then it's too late.

If you get the change the day it happens, someone can update the card in 10 minutes. At week 6, nobody remembers or cares.
