Ran the numbers on 6 months of change data from KompWatch.

Pricing page edits cluster on two days: Wednesday mid-morning and Friday mid-afternoon. Specifically.

Not sure if that's a SaaS marketing calendar thing or confirmation bias in our sample size. Either way — if you're doing your Monday manual check, you're consistently 2–5 days late on the changes that actually matter.

That's the whole problem I built this to fix. Timing, not just coverage.
