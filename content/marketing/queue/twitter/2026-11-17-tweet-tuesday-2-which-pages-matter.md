The hardest part about competitor monitoring isn't the technology.

It's deciding which 3 pages actually matter.

Pricing page — obvious. Features/product page — yes. Homepage — maybe.

Blog, careers, docs, changelog? Pick ONE and commit.

Watching everything = signal buried in noise.
