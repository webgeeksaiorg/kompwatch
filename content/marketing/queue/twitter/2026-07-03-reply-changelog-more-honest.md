Their changelog is more honest than their Twitter.

Feature announcements get spun. Changelogs don't. "Fixed edge case in billing" tells you they had a billing bug that was loud enough to prioritize. "Added CSV export" tells you enterprise prospects kept asking for it.

Watch the changelog. Not the marketing feed.
