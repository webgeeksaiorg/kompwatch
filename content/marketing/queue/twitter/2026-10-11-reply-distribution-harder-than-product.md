Distribution being harder than product is the most under-discussed part of building in public.

Everyone posts their GitHub commit graphs and their launch day numbers. Almost nobody posts "I've had the same 8 users for 3 weeks and I don't know why."

The problem usually isn't the product. It's that the right people don't know it exists.

I'm still figuring out what works for KompWatch. SEO is slow but compounding. Reddit is working but doesn't scale. Most other channels haven't clicked yet.
