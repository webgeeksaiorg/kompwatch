You don't need an analyst. You need a list.

Pick the 3 pages that actually matter for each competitor:
1. Pricing page
2. Features/product page
3. Changelog or blog

Set up something to watch those 6-9 URLs. Anything. Google Alerts, a cron job, KompWatch, whatever.

Review when something changes — not on a schedule.

That's 80% of what enterprise CI teams do. Without the $500/mo tool.
