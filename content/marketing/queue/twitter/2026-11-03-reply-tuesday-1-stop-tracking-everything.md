Honestly the biggest thing that helped was stopping trying to track everything.

Pick 4-5 pages per competitor max. Pricing, features, homepage hero, /alternatives (if they have one). That's it.

The rest — blog, press releases, social — is noise unless you have dedicated CI headcount. Which most of us don't.

Simple system that runs daily beats comprehensive system you do whenever.
