Three months into building KompWatch I thought the homepage was the most important page to monitor.

Wrong. Homepage is marketing theater. Changes quarterly.

The pages that actually move:
- Pricing (especially footnotes + plan limits)
- /features or /product (quiet capability additions)
- Job listings (tells you the roadmap before they announce it)
- Alternatives / compare pages (they're watching you too)

Homepage is the last place a real signal lands.
