Three pages I watch on every competitor:

1. /pricing — first place they telegraph a strategic shift
2. /customers — logo adds/removes tell you where they're winning
3. /changelog — what they're actually shipping vs what they're announcing

Most teams only check the homepage. That's where you get the marketing spin, not the signal.

Set up change alerts on those three and you'll know more than 90% of teams with "CI programs."
