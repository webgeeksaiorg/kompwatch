Don't try to quantify lost deals from missed competitor moves. Nobody can prove that math.

Instead: "Here's what we learned last month that changed our positioning/roadmap/sales deck."

Concrete examples beat ROI calculators every time. One "we saw their pricing change 3 days before the client asked" story is worth more than a spreadsheet.
