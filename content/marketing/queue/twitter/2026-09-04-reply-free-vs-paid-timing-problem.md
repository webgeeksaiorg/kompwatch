For competitor monitoring specifically:

Free layer: Google Alerts (brand/news) + manually checking their pricing page once a month. Works until it doesn't.

When it breaks: you find out a competitor changed their free tier limits by losing a deal where the prospect compared old vs new.

Paid layer solves the "catching it before it matters" problem. Not the tracking itself — the timing.

Whether $49/mo is worth it depends entirely on how often you're in competitive deals.
