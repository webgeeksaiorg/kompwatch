[reply angle: founder asking how much time to spend on competitor research in early stage]

At pre-revenue: 2 hours. Once. Write down their pricing, top 3 features, who they target. Done.

You'll get more signal from 5 customer conversations than 40 hours of competitor research.

Post-revenue: automate the monitoring (so you stop forgetting), spend the 2 hours on the *why* behind whatever changed. That's where the insight is.
