The PM workflow I've seen work: don't do competitive research as a standalone task. Bake it into your sprint retros.

"What did competitors ship this sprint?" becomes a 2-minute agenda item when the alerts already came to your inbox. It's 45 minutes of tab-switching if they didn't.

The tool doesn't matter. The habit does. The tool just makes the habit stick.
