Battlecards only work if they're maintained. And they only get maintained if someone's watching the competitors automatically.

The failure mode I see: a PM writes great battlecards in Q1, nobody updates them, reps are pitching against features that changed in March, it's now September.

The doc isn't the problem. The update workflow is.
