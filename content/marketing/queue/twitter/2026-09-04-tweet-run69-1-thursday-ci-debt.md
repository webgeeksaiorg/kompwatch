There's a concept I've started calling "CI debt."

Every week your team doesn't catch a competitor change, the gap between what you think is true and what's actually true grows. Slowly. Silently.

Battlecard says $299/mo. Competitor switched to $199/mo in July. Your AE is still pitching the wrong comparison in September.

That's 8 weeks of CI debt. And you don't know you owe it until you lose a deal.
