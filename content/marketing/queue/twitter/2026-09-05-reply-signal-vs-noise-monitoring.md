The failure mode with most monitoring tools: they alert on *any* change.

A CSS tweak, a typo fix, a cookie banner update. Signal drowns in noise.

The only things worth alerting on: pricing changes, plan structure changes, CTA text, feature additions. Everything else is markup churn.

Narrow the selector. Tighten the alert. Actually read it.
