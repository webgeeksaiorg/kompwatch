Free ways to track competitor websites, ranked by how long you'll actually stick with it:

1. Browser bookmarks + Monday reminder → 3 weeks
2. Google Alerts for their domain → 2 months, mostly noise
3. Distill.io or VisualPing → solid, until you have 10+ competitors
4. KompWatch (obviously biased) → what I use now

The real answer: whichever one you check on week 12.
