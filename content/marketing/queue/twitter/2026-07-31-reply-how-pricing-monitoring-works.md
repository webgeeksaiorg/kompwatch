We check pricing pages on a configurable cadence — daily by default, hourly on Pro. When the price or the plan structure changes, you get an email with the before/after diff in plain English.

The tricky part is pricing is often behind a login or dynamic. We handle that with CSS selectors so you can target the exact element. Not perfect but covers 80% of cases.
