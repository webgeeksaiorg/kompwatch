The most telling thing a competitor can do to their pricing page:

Remove the price.

"Contact Sales" where a number used to be means: they're going upmarket, they're testing enterprise anchoring, or they decided self-serve was dragging down ACV.

All three of those matter for how you pitch.

Most teams find out three months late. Usually from a prospect.
