Crayon and Klue are built for enterprise PMM teams with budget and headcount to match.

If you're a 10-person company, you don't need a 30-slide competitive intel platform with Salesforce sync. You need to know when your 5 main competitors change their pricing or launch something you should care about.

That's a much simpler problem. And it doesn't cost $500/mo to solve.
