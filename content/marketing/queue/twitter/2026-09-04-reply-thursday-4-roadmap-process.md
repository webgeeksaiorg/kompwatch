Competitive moves belong in your roadmap process, not your inbox.

What I mean: if a competitor ships a feature and your reaction is "oh we should build that," you're already behind. The question is why they built it — what customer pain are they solving that you're not?

Job postings and changelog notes usually tell you more than the feature announcement.
