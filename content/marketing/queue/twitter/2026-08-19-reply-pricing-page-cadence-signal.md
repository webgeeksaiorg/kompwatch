Underrated signal: your competitor's pricing page stops changing.

When a company is growing confidently, they're constantly tweaking pricing — testing anchors, adding tiers, adjusting feature gates. Active experimentation.

When they go quiet? Could mean they're locked in, sure. Or could mean nobody's tending that garden because there are bigger fires.

I watch pricing pages obsessively for this reason. The cadence of changes is as interesting as the changes themselves.
