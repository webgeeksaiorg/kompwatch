this happened to us in week 8.

thought the ICP was "solo founder monitoring their competitive landscape." Turns out nobody in that segment actually paid.

the people who converted: PMs at 20-100 person SaaS companies who had to do competitive research on their own because there's no CI budget.

the signals were in the email open times (4 min vs days later), not the signups. took us 8 weeks to stop looking at the wrong metric.
