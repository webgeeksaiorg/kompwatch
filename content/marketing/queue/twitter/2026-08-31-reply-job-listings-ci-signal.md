Job listings are underrated CI.

3 ML engineer listings from a competitor = they're building something data-heavy. 
2 "Sales Engineer" listings = closing deals that need technical hand-holding = moving upmarket.
0 engineering listings + 2 "Customer Success" = they're in maintenance mode.

You don't need an analyst. You need to look at the right pages.
