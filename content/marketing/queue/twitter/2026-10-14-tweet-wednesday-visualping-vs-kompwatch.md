The tool I see recommended in almost every "how do you track competitor sites" thread is Visualping.

It works. I used it myself for a year.

But it sends you a screenshot diff and says "something changed." That's it. You still have to open the page, figure out what changed, decide if it matters, and update your battlecard manually.

KompWatch reads the diff and tells you: "They added a new pricing tier at $199/mo targeted at teams between 11–50 seats." In plain English.

Different products, really. Visualping is a monitoring layer. We're trying to be a competitive intelligence layer. Still figuring out if there's enough demand for that distinction.
