Every Monday morning, 45 minutes of manual checking. Slack notes to myself that I'd promptly ignore.

Twice I found out about a pricing change from a customer who was already on a call with the competitor.

That was enough. Built the cron job, added the AI summary, named it KompWatch. Still not sexy. Still exactly what I needed.
