The staleness problem is underrated. A battlecard that's 3 months old isn't slightly outdated — in SaaS it's a different product.

What changed our workflow: alerting on the specific pages that feed into battlecards (pricing, features, compare). When the alert fires, one person updates that section. Done in 15 minutes instead of a quarterly review that never happens.

The quarterly review always gets deprioritized. The Slack alert doesn't.
