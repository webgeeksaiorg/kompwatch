Hot take: the most valuable competitor intelligence isn't in their blog posts or press releases.

It's in the tiny stuff. The pricing page where "Starting at $X" quietly becomes "Contact us." The features comparison table where a checkbox disappears. The careers page that suddenly has 8 ML engineer listings.

Those signal real strategic shifts. Usually 3-4 months before the public announcement.

Most teams completely miss them because they're not watching the right pages.
