The ones that matter: pricing structure changes, free tier additions/removals, features that directly overlap your roadmap, "Contact Sales" replacing a self-serve button.

The ones that don't: blog posts, minor copy tweaks, nav reorganization.

I run them through AI classification now — PRICING / FEATURE / MESSAGING / VISUAL. Then ignore everything below HIGH severity unless it's pricing-related.

Went from getting 40 alerts a week to ~5 I actually care about.
