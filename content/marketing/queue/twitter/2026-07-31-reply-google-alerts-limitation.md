Google Alerts for competitors is fine for brand mentions. Completely useless for pricing page changes.

Alerts need something indexable to trigger on. A pricing tier rename, a new feature row in a comparison table, a buried footnote — none of that surfaces.

What it actually misses: everything quiet and deliberate. That's usually the most important stuff.
