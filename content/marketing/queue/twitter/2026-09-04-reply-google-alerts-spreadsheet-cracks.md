Google Alerts + spreadsheet is where most small teams start. Totally valid for early stage.

The cracks show up around month 3:
- Alerts fire for press coverage, not product changes
- Pricing pages are JavaScript — Alerts can't read them
- The spreadsheet stops getting updated because it's nobody's job

We ran this stack for 8 months before I built something automated. The thing that broke it for us was missing a competitor pricing drop mid-quarter. Found out from a prospect, not from our own monitoring.

That was embarrassing enough to fix.
