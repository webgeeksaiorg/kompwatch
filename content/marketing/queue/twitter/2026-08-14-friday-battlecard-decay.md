Battlecards go stale the moment you publish them.

Your competitor updates their pricing. Adds a feature. Changes their target segment. And your sales rep is still using a doc someone wrote 6 months ago with wrong info.

The real problem isn't building battlecards — it's keeping them current.

That's the part nobody talks about.
