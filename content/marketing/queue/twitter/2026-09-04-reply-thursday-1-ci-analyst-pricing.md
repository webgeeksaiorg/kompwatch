The "you need a CI analyst" pitch is real but it's also self-serving pricing logic. 

For most SaaS teams the actual need is: tell me when something changed on my top 5 competitors' pricing, features, or positioning pages. That's a $49/mo problem, not a $45K/year problem.

The analyst layer only makes sense once you have enough signal that summarizing it is a full-time job.
