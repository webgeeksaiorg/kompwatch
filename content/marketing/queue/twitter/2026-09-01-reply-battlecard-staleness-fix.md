battlecards go stale the second after you publish them. not an exaggeration.

the problem isn't that people don't maintain them — it's that nobody finds out the data changed.

the fix we use: monitor the 3 pages per competitor that feed your battlecard (pricing, features, hero message). when they change, the battlecard flag goes red automatically.

human still writes the update. but at least someone knows it's needed.
