Crayon's value prop makes sense at $500k ARR and above, where losing one deal to a competitor costs more than a year of the tool.

Below that? You're paying for features you'll use maybe quarterly. The core need — "tell me when something changed" — is a much simpler problem than the enterprise suite that wraps it.
