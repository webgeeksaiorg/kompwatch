9 months in. Honest answer to "what's the hardest part?"

Not the scraping. Not the AI diffs. Not even getting people to try it.

It's the conversion from "this is useful" to "this is worth $49/mo."

Turns out people will use free monitoring forever. The trigger for paying is a specific moment: you catch something important before it costs you a deal. That moment has to happen before the trial ends.

Everything I'm building now is trying to manufacture that moment faster.
