Battlecards go stale because the update process is manual. Someone has to check the competitor, note the change, and update the doc. That person is usually a PM who has 40 other things to do.

The fix isn't a better battlecard template. It's automating the "detect the change" step so updating the card is a 5-minute task, not a 90-minute research sprint.
