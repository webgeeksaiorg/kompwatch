The process that stuck for me:

1. Every week I get an email digest of what changed on competitor sites (pricing, homepage, feature pages)
2. I tag anything worth flagging for the product team
3. We review them in the Monday product sync

Steps 2 and 3 were always in my calendar. Step 1 used to be manual. Took a while to fix that part.
