The real problem with battlecards isn't that they're hard to build. It's that they go stale the moment you publish them.

Competitor changes pricing 6 weeks after the deck goes out. Nobody updates the card. Rep goes into a demo with wrong numbers.

Battlecards need a refresh trigger. Not a quarterly one. An event-driven one.
