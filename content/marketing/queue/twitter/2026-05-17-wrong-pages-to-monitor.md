Most teams monitoring the wrong competitor pages.

Homepage: almost never changes. Blog: fine, but who cares. About page: useless.

The pages that actually matter:
- Pricing (especially the footnotes)
- Feature comparison / "who is it for"
- Changelog (if public)
- Job listings (signals what they're building)

Pricing footnotes are where the real changes hide. "Billed annually" quietly becoming "billed annually only." Annual lock-in hidden in size-8 text.

I've been watching competitor sites for two years. The homepage barely moves. The pricing page moves a lot — often with no announcement.

Watch the right pages.
