The competitor pages nobody tells you to monitor (but should):

- /careers — tells you what they're building before they announce it
- /changelog — if they have one, your battlecards need weekly refreshes
- /pricing (not just the price, the tier names and feature gates)
- /customers or /case-studies — who they're targeting

Everyone monitors the homepage. Nobody monitors these.
