For a 5-person team, I'd skip the fancy tools entirely.

Three pages per competitor, checked on a schedule: pricing, /customers or case studies, and the changelog or blog. That's it.

90% of meaningful competitor changes show up on those three. Everything else is noise you'll eventually start ignoring anyway.

Once manual checking starts taking more than 30 min/week, that's when automation makes sense.
