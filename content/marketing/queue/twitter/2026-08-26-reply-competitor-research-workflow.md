My process before I built anything:

1. Monday morning: open 6 saved tabs, check for changes manually
2. If something changed: screenshot it, drop in Slack with a note
3. Forget to check for 2-3 weeks
4. See a competitor's LinkedIn post announcing the feature I missed

The tool I built is basically step 1 automated. Everything else is still manual and probably should be. The hard part isn't detection — it's deciding what to do about it.
