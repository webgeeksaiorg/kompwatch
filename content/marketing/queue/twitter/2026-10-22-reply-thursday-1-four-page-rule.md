We monitor ~4-5 pages per competitor: pricing, /features, job listings, and their main alternatives/compare page. That's it.

Everything else is low signal. You end up alert-fatigued chasing blog updates nobody reads.

The 4-page rule cut noise by 80% for the teams using KompWatch. Same signal, way less time staring at diffs.
