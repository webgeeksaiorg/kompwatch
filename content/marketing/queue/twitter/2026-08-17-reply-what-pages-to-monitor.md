[Reply angle: someone asking what pages to actually watch on a competitor site]

Four that actually matter: pricing, features (or /product), the hiring page, and their alternatives/comparison pages.

Pricing = intent to move up or down market. Features = what they're betting on. Hiring = where they're expanding 6-12 months out. Alternatives page = how they're positioning against you right now.

Homepage changes are usually just marketing refreshes. Don't bother.

Blog is noise unless they're publishing very category-specific stuff. Even then it's a lagging indicator.
