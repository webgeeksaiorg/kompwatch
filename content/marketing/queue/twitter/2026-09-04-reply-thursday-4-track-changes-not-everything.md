the thing that took me forever to figure out: you don't need to track everything, you need to track changes.

pricing page looking the same for 6 months? fine, check weekly. they just moved from 3 plans to 5 plans with a new enterprise tier? that's a signal worth acting on right now.

the alert is only useful if it tells you something changed, not just that you checked.
