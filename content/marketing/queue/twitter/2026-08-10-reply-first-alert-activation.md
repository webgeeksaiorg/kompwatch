The hardest part isn't getting people on the free tier.

It's getting them to the moment where they think "oh, this is actually useful" before they forget the tool exists.

For us that's the first alert. When they see a competitor actually changed something they didn't know about.

Before that moment, we're just another free tool they signed up for.
