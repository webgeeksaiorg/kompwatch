Google Alerts is great for press coverage. Does basically nothing for website changes.

It won't tell you when a competitor quietly drops their free plan. Or bumps a plan from $79 to $99. Or adds "enterprise only" to a feature that used to be in the base tier.

Those changes happen on the website, not in articles. GA scans articles.

That's the gap. Website diffing is its own separate thing.
