For tools under $200/mo: PLG or bust. Nobody's approving a purchase order for $49/mo. They just sign up.

The friction you're fighting isn't sales objections. It's onboarding — will someone paste their first competitor URL and get value before they leave?

That's the only question that matters at that price point.
