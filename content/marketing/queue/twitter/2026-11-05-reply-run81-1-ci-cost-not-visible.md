the cost isn't a line item. it's the deals you lose and don't know why.

rep quotes price, prospect says "that's higher than [competitor]." rep doesn't know what changed last week. deal slips.

multiply that by how often your competitor changes pricing. for most SaaS companies it's 2–4x per year.

the CI gap shows up in win rates, not invoices.
