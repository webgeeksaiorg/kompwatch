Win-loss interviews are gold if you can do them consistently.

Most teams can't. One deal a month, engineers busy, nobody follows up.

The gap I see most often: teams *want* win-loss data but settle for "our competitor changed something and we found out 6 weeks later in a sales call."

Monitoring the competitor's own pages doesn't replace win-loss. But it closes the gap between deals.
