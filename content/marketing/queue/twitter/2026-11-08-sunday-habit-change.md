Sunday night I used to get a low-grade dread knowing Monday morning meant 45 minutes of tab-switching through competitor websites.

Pricing pages. Features pages. Hiring pages. Homepage copy.

Twelve tabs. Weekly. For years.

The worst part wasn't the time. It was knowing I was probably missing changes that happened on a Thursday.

Built KompWatch mostly to fix my own Sunday evenings. Still weird to think that's now a product.
