Our ICP took 8 weeks to get right.

Started broad: "anyone who tracks competitors." Useless. Everyone tracks competitors. Nobody agreed their current process was broken.

Narrowed to: PMs at 10-50 person SaaS companies who are currently doing it manually in a spreadsheet and losing deals because their info is 3 months old.

Tiny segment. Very specific pain. They convert.

The "manual spreadsheet" qualifier is the one that matters. If they're already using a tool, I'm competing. If they're using a spreadsheet, I'm upgrading.
