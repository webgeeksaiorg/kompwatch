What I actually use, in order of ROI:

1. KompWatch — daily snapshot of 8 pages per competitor (yes, my own tool)
2. LinkedIn Company Pages — hiring signals
3. Syften — Reddit/HN mentions
4. Literally nothing else

The $500/mo enterprise stack makes sense when you have a full CI team. For a 10-person company? Three tools, $80/mo total.
