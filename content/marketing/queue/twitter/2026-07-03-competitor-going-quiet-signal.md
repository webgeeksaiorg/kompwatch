Underrated CI signal: when a competitor goes quiet.

No new changelog entries. Careers page stops posting eng roles. Blog goes from twice a week to nothing.

That's not a company coasting. That's a pivot in progress, a fundraise going sideways, or a reorg happening above the product team.

Changes to the website tell you what they shipped. Absence of changes tells you something else entirely.
