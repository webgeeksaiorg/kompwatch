Battlecards going stale is almost always a data pipeline problem, not a writing problem.

If the underlying monitoring isn't automatic, you're doing calendar-driven CI. Someone has to remember to update it. They won't.

Push alerts into wherever sales lives. The battlecard updates itself or it doesn't update.
