The gap between "I have a cron job that diffs pages" and "I have a product people pay for" took me about 4 months.

Turns out people don't want a diff. They want to know: did anything important change? Those are different problems.

The diff is easy. The "was this important" is where you need AI.

(Still figuring out the "was this actually actionable" layer tbh.)
