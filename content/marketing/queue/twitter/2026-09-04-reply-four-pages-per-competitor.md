I watch 4 pages per competitor: pricing, features/product, homepage hero, and their careers page.

That's it. Not their blog (too slow), not their social (too noisy), not their press releases (marketing fluff).

Pricing and features tell you what they're selling. Homepage tells you how they're positioning. Careers tells you where they're investing next.

Everything else is noise.
