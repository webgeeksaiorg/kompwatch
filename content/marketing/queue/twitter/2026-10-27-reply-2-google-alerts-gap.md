Google Alerts catches news mentions. Doesn't catch:

- Pricing page rewrites (no press release)
- Free tier removal (no announcement)
- "Contact Sales" replacing the price you had publicly listed
- New competitor in the comparison table they added to their homepage

The stuff that actually matters usually doesn't make the news. It just quietly appears on their website.
