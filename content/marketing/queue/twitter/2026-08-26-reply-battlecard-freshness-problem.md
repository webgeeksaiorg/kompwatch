Battlecards are only as good as the freshness of the data in them.

I've seen teams with great battlecard templates that were 8 months out of date. The competitor had repriced, launched a new feature, and changed their main CTA — none of it reflected.

The hard problem isn't the battlecard format. It's building the system that keeps them current.
