Churn at day 7 usually isn't about the product — it's about whether they got value before they got confused.

My first churned trial didn't add a competitor URL. Didn't get a single alert. Zero value delivered.

The entire week 1 experience needs to be about getting one alert in front of them. Nothing else matters until that happens.

Activation moment beats feature set every time.
