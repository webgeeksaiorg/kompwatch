We track: pricing page, features page, homepage hero, job listings.

That's it. Everything else — blog, case studies, social — is signal dilution if you're a small team.

Pricing + features tells you what they're selling. Hero tells you who they're selling to. Jobs tells you where they're investing.

Four pages, automated, daily. That's an actual system.
