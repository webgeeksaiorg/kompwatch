[reply angle: someone asking if building in public actually helps with distribution]

It helps with one specific thing: forcing you to be honest with yourself.

When you have to post "week 8, 0 paid customers" publicly, you can't pretend the ICP is fine. You figure it out faster.

Distribution benefit is real but slow. Accountability benefit is immediate.
