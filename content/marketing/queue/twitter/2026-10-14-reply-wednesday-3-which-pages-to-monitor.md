The three pages that have never let me down:

1. /pricing — obvious, but watch the page itself not just the numbers. Tier names, feature bullets, FAQ text
2. /compare or /alternatives — this is where they telegraph their positioning
3. Job postings filtered to eng/infra — if they're hiring 4 ML engineers in 30 days, something's shipping

Homepage hero: overrated. It changes less than people think. Check quarterly.

Blog: almost never actionable for CI. Skip it.
