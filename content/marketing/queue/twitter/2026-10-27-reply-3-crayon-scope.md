Crayon is genuinely good if you need a dedicated analyst workflow with integrations into your CRM, Slack, battlecard system, etc.

The problem: they're priced and designed for that use case. $500/mo+ means you need someone whose job it is to extract value from the tool.

If you just want "tell me when a competitor's pricing or product page changes" — that's a much smaller problem. KompWatch does that for $49/mo.

Different scope entirely.
