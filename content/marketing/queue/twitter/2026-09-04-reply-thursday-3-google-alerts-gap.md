Google Alerts is great for news mentions. It's pretty bad for website changes.

It won't catch: pricing tier removal, "Contact Sales" replacing a price, free plan getting killed quietly, features added to a plan without an announcement, job listings.

That's the stuff that actually matters in a competitive deal. The blog post about their new partnership doesn't.
