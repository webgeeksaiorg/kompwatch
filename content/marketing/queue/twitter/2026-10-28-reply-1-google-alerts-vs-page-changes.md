For pure website changes — pricing, features, copy updates — we watch the actual pages with headless browser snapshots. The difference: Google Alerts catches press releases and news. It misses when a competitor quietly removes their free tier or changes "Contact Sales" from $200/mo to $299/mo at 2am.

Those silent website edits are the ones that matter most before a demo.
