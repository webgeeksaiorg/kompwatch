Monday morning ritual.

Open 6 browser tabs. Check each competitor's pricing page. Scroll changelog if they have one. Note anything different. Close tabs.

Repeat next Monday.

I did this for 2 years before building something to do it for me. The embarrassing part: I still missed changes. Wednesday deploys. Weekend updates. Stuff that slipped through the gap.

The manual process doesn't fail because you're not trying. It fails because you're human and you can't check every day.
