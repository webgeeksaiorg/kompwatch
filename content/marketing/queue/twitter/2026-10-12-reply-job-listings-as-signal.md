This is genuinely underused.

Competitor posts 4 senior ML engineers in one week → they're building an AI feature. Six months before it ships.

Posts a Head of Enterprise Sales → pricing tier expansion incoming.

Hiring freeze + no product updates → acquisition prep or runway problems.

Job listings are the only honest signal. Nobody writes accurate press releases about where they're going, but everyone posts their real problems on LinkedIn.
