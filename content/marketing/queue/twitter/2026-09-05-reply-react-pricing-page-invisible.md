The part nobody talks about: React pricing pages are invisible to most monitoring tools.

Google Alerts reads the HTML shell. VisualPing renders a screenshot. Neither tells you what the page *says* after JavaScript runs.

Headless browser is the only way to actually capture what a visitor sees. Took me 3 weeks of breakage to figure this out.
