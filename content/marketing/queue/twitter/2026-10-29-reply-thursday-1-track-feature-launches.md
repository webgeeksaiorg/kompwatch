Low-tech version that works: bookmark their /changelog, /release-notes, and /whats-new pages. Check weekly.

Higher-leverage version: automate the snapshot + diff so you get emailed when they change. That's literally what I built KompWatch for. You just paste the URLs, it does the watching.

The manual version works until you have more than 2 competitors and a real job.
