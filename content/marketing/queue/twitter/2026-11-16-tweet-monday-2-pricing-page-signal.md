one of the most underrated signals in SaaS: when a competitor quietly removes a plan tier

no announcement. no blog post. just gone.

usually means:
- they couldn't monetize that segment
- they're moving upmarket
- churn from that tier was too high

your pricing page is a strategy doc. theirs is too.
