Battlecards go stale in ways nobody notices until a rep loses a deal.

The competitor's pricing page changed 3 months ago. The battlecard still shows the old numbers. Rep uses the wrong objection handler. Deal gone.

The problem isn't that teams don't update battlecards. It's that there's no signal telling them to.

Need the alert first. Then the update becomes obvious.
