The ROI conversation for competitor monitoring always goes the same way:

"How do you justify $49/mo?"

Simple: one deal you don't lose because you had current intel pays for 2+ years of the tool.

The harder question is the one nobody asks: what does 6 months of *not knowing* your competitor killed their free tier and went upmarket cost you in stale battlecards, lost deals, and reps who sound out-of-date on a call?

The absence has a price too. It's just invisible.
