No CI analyst on the team? Same situation I was in.

What I do: 5 pages per competitor (pricing, features, homepage, /alternatives, careers). Daily snapshot. AI summarizes changes. Takes 10 minutes to set up, then it runs on its own.

That's it. You don't need a dedicated analyst for a 4-page watch list.
