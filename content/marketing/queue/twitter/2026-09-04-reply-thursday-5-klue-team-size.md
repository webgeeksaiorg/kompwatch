Klue is built for a dedicated CI team with a manager, an analyst, and a process. If you have that, it's probably worth it.

If you're a PM who needs to know what your 3–5 main competitors are doing week to week, it's a $20K/year warehouse for a 3-shelf closet.

Happy to share what we're building instead — much smaller scope, $49/mo.
