Google Alerts catches press mentions. It misses the actual product changes.

Competitor kills their free tier? No press release. Just a pricing page update on a Wednesday.

Competitor adds a new enterprise plan? A changelog no journalist is writing about.

Alerts is good for reputation monitoring. Not for tracking what they're actually doing to their product.
