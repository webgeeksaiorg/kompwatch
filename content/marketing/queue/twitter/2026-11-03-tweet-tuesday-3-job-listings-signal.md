If your competitor just posted 5 ML engineer jobs — that's a product announcement.

They're not going to blog about it for another 6 months. But the jobs are there right now.

Job listings tell you:
- What they're building (skill requirements)
- When they're building it (volume + urgency)
- Where they're struggling (backfill roles)

This is the cheapest market research you can do. Most people don't bother to check.
