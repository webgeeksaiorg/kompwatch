The process that's worked for me:

1. Pick 4-5 competitors you actually lose deals to (not aspirational ones)
2. For each: pricing page, feature/product page, /alternatives page
3. Monitor those 12-15 pages on a daily schedule
4. When something changes, route the alert to whoever owns the battlecard
5. That person has one job: update the doc within 24 hours

The AI summary is the diff. The human is the decision-maker.

Took me 3 weeks to build. Takes users 30 minutes to set up with KompWatch.
