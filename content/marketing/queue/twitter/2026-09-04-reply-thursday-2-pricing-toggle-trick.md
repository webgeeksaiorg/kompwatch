Watch the annual plan toggle, not just the headline number.

Competitors change their effective price by adjusting the annual discount, adding seats to mid-tier plans, or quietly moving a feature from a lower tier to the higher one.

None of that changes the big number. All of it changes the actual cost comparison.

If you're only screenshotting the pricing page monthly you're going to miss the subtle stuff.
