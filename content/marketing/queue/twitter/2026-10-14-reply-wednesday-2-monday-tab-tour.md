We called this the "Monday tab tour" internally. 6 tabs pinned. Open each one, scan for changes, close it, try to remember if something looked different last week.

The problem isn't the tool — it's that human memory is a terrible diff algorithm.

I ran the Monday tab tour for 2 years before admitting I was missing 70% of the changes just by checking on the wrong day.
