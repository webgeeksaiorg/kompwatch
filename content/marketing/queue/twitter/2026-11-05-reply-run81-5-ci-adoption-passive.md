the tool isn't the problem. the workflow is.

if CI lives in a Notion doc that gets updated quarterly, nobody reads it. if it shows up in their inbox on Monday morning summarizing what changed last week, they read it every time.

make the consumption passive. the research should come to them, not sit somewhere they have to remember to go look.
