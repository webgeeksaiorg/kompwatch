Product teams are bad at competitive intel for one reason: it's not urgent until it is.

Nobody cares about the competitor's pricing page until a deal dies because your AE quoted the wrong number.

Nobody cares about the feature changelog until a prospect says "but [Competitor] just shipped that."

The urgency comes AFTER the damage. By then you're reactive.

The fix isn't a $500/mo enterprise platform. It's just... knowing what changed before the call.
