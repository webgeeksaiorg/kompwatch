There's a category of competitor move that looks big but isn't — and one that looks small but is.

New pricing page layout = usually just a redesign. Quiet addition of an enterprise tier = worth paying attention to.

You learn to read the signals after a while. Or you automate the detection and let yourself focus on interpretation.
