Google Alerts is fine for news mentions.

It won't catch: pricing tier removal, free plan killed, feature quietly dropped, "Contact Sales" button replacing self-serve signup.

Those changes happen on the website, not in press releases. Alerts indexes news. Doesn't diff HTML.

Burned us once — competitor dropped their free tier on a Thursday. We found out Monday. Sales call Friday had been rough.
