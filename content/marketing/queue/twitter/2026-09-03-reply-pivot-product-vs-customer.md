[reply angle: debate about when a startup should pivot vs. stay the course]

The distinction I'd make: pivoting the product vs. pivoting the customer.

Pivoting the product is expensive. Takes months. High risk.

Pivoting who you're selling to is cheap. Takes a week to rewrite the landing page.

I'm doing the second thing right now. Same product. New ICP. Much lower stakes.
