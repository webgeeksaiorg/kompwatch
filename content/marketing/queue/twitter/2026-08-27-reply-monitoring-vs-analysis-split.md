One thing that helped us: separate "monitoring" from "analysis."

Monitoring is automated. Tool watches the pages. Alerts you when something changes.

Analysis is 20 minutes on Thursday morning, reading what changed and deciding if it matters.

The mistake is trying to do both manually.
