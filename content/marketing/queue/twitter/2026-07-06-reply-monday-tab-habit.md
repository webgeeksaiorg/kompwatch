I did this for 2 years. 6 tabs, every Monday, before my first meeting.

The problem wasn't the time. It was the mid-week changes I missed completely.

Competitor dropped their price on Thursday. I found out on the following Tuesday when a prospect mentioned it.

Automated it with KompWatch. Now I get a digest instead of tabs.
