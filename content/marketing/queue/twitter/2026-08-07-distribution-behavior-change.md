The hardest thing about building a competitor monitoring tool isn't the scraping.

It's that your target customers are watching their competitors, not you.

They're not searching for solutions. They're stuck in habits: Google Alerts, Monday tab ritual, quarterly Klue screenshots nobody reads.

Distribution is a behavior change problem. Product is the easy part.

Still figuring out the former.
