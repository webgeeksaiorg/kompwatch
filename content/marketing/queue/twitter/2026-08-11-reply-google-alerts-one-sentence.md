The Google Alerts / website monitoring distinction in one sentence:

Google Alerts fires when someone writes *about* your competitor.

Website monitoring fires when your competitor changes their own site.

Those are completely different signals. One is the press. One is the source.

Your competitor's pricing page change will almost never trigger a Google Alert.
