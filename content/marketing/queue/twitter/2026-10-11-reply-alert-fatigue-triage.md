Alert fatigue in competitor monitoring is almost always a triage problem, not a volume problem.

If you're getting 30 alerts a week and most of them don't matter, the fix isn't reducing monitoring frequency. It's ignoring low-signal pages.

Blog posts, case studies, navigation changes, press releases: almost never worth alerting on. Pricing, features comparison, /vs/ pages, changelog: almost always worth alerting on.

Once I had that mental model clear, the noise dropped dramatically even though I was watching the same number of pages.
