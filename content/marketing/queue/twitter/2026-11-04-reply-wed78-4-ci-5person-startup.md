at 5 people you don't need a strategy doc. you need:

1. 3-4 URLs per competitor to watch (pricing, features, jobs)
2. something that pings you when they change
3. a 10-minute weekly "what changed this week?" conversation

that's it. seriously. the $25K Crayon playbook is for teams with dedicated analysts. at your size, 80% of the value comes from just not missing the obvious stuff.
