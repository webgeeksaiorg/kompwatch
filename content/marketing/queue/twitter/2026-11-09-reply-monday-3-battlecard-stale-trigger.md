Battlecards go stale the same way milk does — there's no warning, then one day they're completely wrong.

The only fix is a signal that tells you when to update them. Otherwise you're on a quarterly review cycle hoping nothing important changed in Q2.

We trigger battlecard review in our workflow whenever KompWatch flags a HIGH or MEDIUM change on the competitor's features or pricing page.
