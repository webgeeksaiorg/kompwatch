biggest surprise so far building KompWatch in public:

people don't tell you your product is bad. they tell you their workaround.

"oh I just check manually every Monday" — that IS the feedback. that's the whole product story right there.

the building-in-public stuff matters less than I expected. the conversations in comments matter more. that's where the real product research lives.
