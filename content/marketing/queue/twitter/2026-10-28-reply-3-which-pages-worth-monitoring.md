Honest answer from 6 months of data:

Worth monitoring: pricing page, features/compare page, homepage hero (especially above-fold copy), /alternatives or /vs pages if they exist, job listings (signals what they're investing in next).

Waste of time: blog, about page, news/press room. High volume, low signal. You'll get alert fatigue in week 2 and stop checking.

The job listing thing surprises people. 5 ML engineer postings = they're building AI features. That's 3-6 months advance notice.
