The CI problem isn't finding information. It's knowing when to stop.

You can track 20 competitors across 10 channels each and generate 200 signals a week. At that point "competitive intelligence" is indistinguishable from "extra inbox noise."

The constraint that made our CI useful: 5 competitors. 3 pages each. One digest twice a week.

Fewer sources → more consistent reads → actual pattern recognition.
