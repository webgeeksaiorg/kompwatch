Going through exactly this with KompWatch right now.

What I've figured out: the activation moment isn't "set up monitoring." It's "catch something that mattered before it would have cost you."

People need to experience value before they pay. For competitor monitoring, that means they have to get an alert on something real during the trial window.

So now I'm focused less on onboarding and more on: can I get each new user to monitor pages that actually change? Because if nothing changes during the trial, they'll never convert.
