My Monday morning ritual for two years: 12 browser tabs, manually scanning competitor pricing pages and changelog posts.

I kept a Google Sheet. Highlighted cells when I spotted a change. Shared it with the team in Slack.

The sheet was always behind by 3-5 days. The tab for one competitor was dead for 6 weeks before I noticed they'd migrated their pricing page.

This is what I built KompWatch to kill.
