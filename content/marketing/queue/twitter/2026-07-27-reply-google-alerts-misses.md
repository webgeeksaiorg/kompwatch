Google Alerts works for one thing: when someone writes *about* your competitor.

It completely misses:
- Pricing page changes
- Feature additions buried in a changelog
- Removed pages (very telling)
- New /compare pages targeting your product
- Trial CTA copy tests

That's the stuff that actually affects your deals. None of it gets indexed fast enough for Alerts to help.
