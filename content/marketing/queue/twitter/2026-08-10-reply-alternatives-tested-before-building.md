Tested a bunch of approaches before building one myself:

- Google Alerts: catches news mentions, not website changes
- Visualping: good for visual diffs, doesn't tell you if it's a pricing change vs a typo fix
- Manual checking: lasts about 3 weeks before you forget

What I actually needed was: change detected → classified (pricing/feature/messaging) → summarized in plain English.

Took 3 months to build that last bit correctly.
