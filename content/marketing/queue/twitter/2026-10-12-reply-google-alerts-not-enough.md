Google Alerts for competitor monitoring is like using the smoke detector to monitor your fridge.

It catches news mentions. Press coverage. Blog posts they publish.

It doesn't catch: pricing tier quietly changed, free plan killed, "Contact Sales" button added where self-serve used to be.

Those silent page edits are the ones that matter most. They never trigger an alert.
