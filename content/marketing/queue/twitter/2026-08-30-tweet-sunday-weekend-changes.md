Competitors don't take weekends off.

I checked the data on KompWatch: 34% of pricing page changes happen Thursday-Sunday.

Which means your Monday review is catching changes that are 3-4 days old.

By then your competitor's sales team has already adjusted their pitch.

Monitoring once a week is better than nothing. Real-time is better than that.
