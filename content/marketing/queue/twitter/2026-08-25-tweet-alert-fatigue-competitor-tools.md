The problem with most competitor alert tools isn't that they miss changes.

It's that they email you every change.

New blog post. Image swap. Footer link update. Navigation reorder. All equally "urgent."

After a week you stop reading them. That's not monitoring. That's a new kind of noise.

The only thing that matters: did their pricing change, did a feature ship, did they update the homepage for a new ICP. Everything else is background.
