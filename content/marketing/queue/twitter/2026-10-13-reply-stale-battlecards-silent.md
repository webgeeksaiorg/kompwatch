The problem isn't that battlecards go stale. It's that they go stale silently.

There's no notification. Nobody tells your reps "hey, that free tier you've been using as a differentiator? They killed it 6 weeks ago."

The rep goes into the call with wrong info. They find out from the prospect. That's a trust hit you can't undo.

24-hour change alerts specifically so this doesn't happen. It's the whole point.
