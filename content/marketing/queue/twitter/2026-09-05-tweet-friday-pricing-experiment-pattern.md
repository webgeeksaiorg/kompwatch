One thing I've noticed watching 50+ SaaS pricing pages for 8 months:

Companies almost never raise prices suddenly. They test first. Add a new tier. Remove a feature from free. Shrink the trial window.

By the time they announce the price hike, they've been running the experiment for 6 weeks.

That's the change worth catching.
