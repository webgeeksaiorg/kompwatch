if you're just starting out, start with four per competitor:

1. pricing page — changes signal product and business model moves
2. features/product page — where new capabilities appear before they're announced
3. docs — new sections = new features, usually no announcement lag
4. careers — 60-day leading indicator for where their roadmap is heading

homepage and blog are worth having but way noisier. get signal first.
