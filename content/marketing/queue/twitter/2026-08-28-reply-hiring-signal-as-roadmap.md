Competitor job postings are underrated as a CI signal.

Posted 3 senior ML engineer roles → they're building AI features (6-9 months out).

Posted 4 enterprise AE roles → they're moving upmarket.

0 engineering, 5 customer success → scaling existing product, not shipping new stuff.

You can often read the roadmap from the careers page. Way cheaper than Klue.
