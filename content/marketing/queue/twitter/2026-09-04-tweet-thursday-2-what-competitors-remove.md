Something nobody talks about in CI: what your competitors *removed*.

Added a plan tier? You'll see that. New feature callout on the homepage? Obvious.

But the deletion of the "Teams" plan footnote. The support chat widget that vanished. The "cancel anytime" guarantee that quietly disappeared from the checkout page.

That's signal. Quiet signal, but loud if you know what to look for.

Removing things takes a board decision. Pay attention.
