The hardest part of competitor monitoring isn't detecting changes.

It's knowing which ones to care about.

Rough priority order I've settled on after 5 months of watching this stuff:

1. Pricing page (tier structure changes, not formatting)
2. /vs/ and /alternatives pages (they're writing against you publicly)
3. Changelog (what they just shipped)
4. Careers (what they're building next)
5. Homepage hero (how they're repositioning)

Blog posts, case studies, nav tweaks: almost always ignore.

Most teams have this backwards.
