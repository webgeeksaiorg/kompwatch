Job postings are underrated for this.

A competitor posting 4 ML engineer roles tells you more about their next 6-month roadmap than anything on their website. Hiring signals are public and they can't hide them.

Combine that with what's changing on their /features page and /changelog, and you've got a reasonable directional read without buying Crayon.
