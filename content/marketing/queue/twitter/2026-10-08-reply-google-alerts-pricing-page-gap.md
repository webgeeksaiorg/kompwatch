Google Alerts catches press mentions and blog posts. Useful for brand monitoring.

What it doesn't catch: when they quietly swap "$29/mo" to "$39/mo" on the pricing page. Or when the free tier disappears. Or when they add a new feature to the feature comparison table.

Those changes happen on pages that don't generate new URLs. Google never re-indexes them in time.
