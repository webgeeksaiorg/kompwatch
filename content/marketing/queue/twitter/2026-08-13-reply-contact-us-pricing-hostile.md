"Contact us for pricing" is the most hostile UX pattern in B2B software and somehow it keeps spreading.

The competitor angle is real too: when you go dark on pricing, your competitors monitor your job listings and customer count on LinkedIn to estimate your ACVs anyway. You're not hiding anything. You're just frustrating buyers.

Watched three of our tracked competitors flip from public to gated pricing this year. All three saw inbound traffic to their pricing pages drop. Still worth it to them apparently.
