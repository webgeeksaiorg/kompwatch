[Reply angle: AE or sales ops person saying they lost a deal because they didn't know about a competitor feature/pricing change]

This is the most expensive version of "we didn't know."

The information existed. It was on their website. Nobody had eyes on it that day.

Pricing changes, free tier kills, a new enterprise package — stuff like that lives on the competitor's pricing page for weeks before it costs you a deal. The gap isn't research, it's timing.

Knowing 3 hours before the call is different from knowing 3 days after.
