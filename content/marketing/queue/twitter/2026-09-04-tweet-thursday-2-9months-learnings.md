9 months building KompWatch. what actually surprised me:

— most teams check 3-5 competitor pages max. they think they're checking more
— pricing pages change ~40% of the time in any given month across the users we track
— nobody reads the full HTML diff. they just want "what changed, in plain English"
— the hardest part wasn't the scraper. it was convincing people to stop doing it manually

still figuring out the growth side tbh. the monitoring part works.
