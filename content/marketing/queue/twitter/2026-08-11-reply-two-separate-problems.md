Three months pre-revenue: the thing nobody tells you is that "does the product work" and "can people find it" are completely separate problems.

Product works. CI data accurate. Digests useful. Scraper handles SPAs.

Zero organic discovery. Because I was the only person manually checking competitor sites all Monday — and I automated that away before building any distribution.

Lesson: solve distribution before automating your own sourcing.
