honestly the no-budget version is: pick 4 pages per competitor (pricing, features, homepage hero, /alternatives or /compare). check them weekly. take notes in a doc.

where it breaks down is the "check them weekly" part. life happens. you miss 3 weeks, then it's 3 months.

automation is mostly for solving the "you'll never actually do this consistently" problem.
