Short answer: 4 pages per competitor, that's it.

1. /pricing — obvious
2. /features or /product — second most important
3. /alternatives or /compare — this is where they telegraph positioning
4. Career page or /jobs — hiring signals are product signals

Homepage is a trap. Changes there are usually marketing copy tweaks with zero competitive relevance. Blog posts are fine but don't prioritize them.

If you're checking more than 4 pages per competitor you're probably creating noise, not intelligence.
