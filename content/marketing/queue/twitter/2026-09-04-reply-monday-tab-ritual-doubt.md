I had 6 tabs pinned. Every Monday morning, check each one manually, screenshot, compare to last week's mental image.

I missed a pricing change for three weeks because I wasn't sure if I was misremembering, or if it actually changed. It had changed. I just had no diff to prove it.

The mental overhead of manual monitoring is underrated as a problem. It's not the 20 minutes checking. It's the doubt.
