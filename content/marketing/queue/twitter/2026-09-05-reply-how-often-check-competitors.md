Honestly? Once I stopped asking "how often" and started asking "how quickly" — the whole thing clicked.

The real question isn't frequency. It's: how fast do you find out when something changes?

If your competitor drops a free tier on Monday and you're pitching against them Friday, that's a 4-day window you're selling blind.
