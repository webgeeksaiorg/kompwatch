The onboarding mistake I made: I optimized for "adding competitors" as the activation event.

People added competitors and then left. Because adding a competitor doesn't feel like the product working. It feels like setup.

The activation event is the FIRST ALERT. That's when it clicks. That's when they understand what they paid for.

Every flow I've built since points directly at "get them to an alert in 24 hours."
