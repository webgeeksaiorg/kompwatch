Spent 3 weeks learning which competitor page changes actually matter.

Pricing page: obviously.

But the real signal is what's *not* there anymore. Free tier disappears quietly. Annual discount shrinks from 30% to 20%. "Contact us for pricing" replaces the number.

None of that shows up in Google Alerts.
