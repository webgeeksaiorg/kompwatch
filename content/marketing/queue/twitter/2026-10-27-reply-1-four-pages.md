Pricing page, features/product page, homepage hero, and careers/jobs section.

That's it. Four URLs per competitor. Everything else is noise.

Pricing tells you strategy shifts. Features tells you roadmap. Hero copy tells you who they're targeting now. Jobs tells you what they're building next.

I check these on a 24hr cycle via KompWatch ($49/mo). Before that it was a Monday morning tab ritual that I'd skip half the time.
