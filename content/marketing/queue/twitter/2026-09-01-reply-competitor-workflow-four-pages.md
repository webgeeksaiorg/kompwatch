honest answer from someone who built a tool for this:

the workflow that actually sticks is: pick 4-5 pages per competitor (pricing, features, alternatives page, careers), get an alert when they change, read the diff.

that's it. not a 30-page battlecard. not weekly "CI debrief" meetings.

the teams that track 40 pages per competitor get overwhelmed and stop checking. the ones who pick 5 stay on top of it.
