Pre-PMF? Probably not worth paying $500+/mo for.

But "don't pay much for it" ≠ "ignore it entirely." There's a middle ground:

Monitor 2-3 direct competitors manually. Pricing page, features page, changelog. 15 minutes every 2 weeks. Write it in a doc nobody reads. At least you'll know when something big shifts.

Post-PMF when you have sales reps losing deals to those competitors — that's when automated monitoring pays for itself in the first month.

The mistake is doing nothing pre-PMF and then scrambling to set up intelligence infrastructure while also running a sales motion. That's when things fall through cracks.
