Yes, job listings are one of the best early signals. Three ML engineer postings = something's coming. New "Head of Enterprise" role = they're going upmarket.

The hard part is doing it consistently. Manual LinkedIn checks fall off after week 2.

We added job listing monitoring to KompWatch because I kept forgetting to check. Now it's just in the weekly digest alongside everything else.
