their blog and changelog are the obvious places but most changes don't show up there first.

pricing page footnotes, feature comparison tables, the actual /features page copy — that's where stuff lands before the announcement post. sometimes days before.

we've caught pricing tier restructures, free seat limits changing, feature gating tweaks — none of them announced, all of them live on the page.
