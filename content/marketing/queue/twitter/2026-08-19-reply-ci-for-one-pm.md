Most CI advice assumes you have a dedicated team and a $50K budget.

Reality for most startups: it's one PM doing this on the side, maybe one hour a week.

For those teams, the question isn't "which enterprise platform do we buy" — it's "what can I automate so I stop missing stuff?"

The answer is usually: just watch the URLs that matter. Pricing, features, integrations, careers. When any of them changes, you want to know same-day, not next Monday.

That's the 80% use case that nobody builds for. (We're building for it.)
