Google Alerts is fine for news mentions. Misses almost everything that actually matters.

It doesn't see a pricing page add a footnote. Doesn't catch when /features quietly drops a capability. Doesn't notice a "Contact Sales" button replacing a self-serve signup.

That stuff is where the real signal is. Happens in the HTML, not in press releases.
