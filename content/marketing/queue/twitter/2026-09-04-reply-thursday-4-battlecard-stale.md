The part nobody tells you about battlecards: they go stale silently.

You write a great one in January. By March, the competitor has repositioned, changed pricing, and added two features. Your sales team is still using the January version.

Automated monitoring isn't just about alerts — it's about knowing when to reopen the battlecard doc.
