The hardest competitor change to respond to isn't a price increase.

It's when they remove a free tier or heavily restrict a trial.

Because it means they've figured out their ideal customer doesn't need a free tier to convert. That's a confidence signal — and it usually means they're making more money than you think.

Worth knowing before your next pricing review.
