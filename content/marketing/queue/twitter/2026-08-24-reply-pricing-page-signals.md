Pricing page is the first thing I monitor for any competitor. Not because prices change that often — but because when they do, it tells you everything.

A price drop means pressure. A price increase means confidence. New "Enterprise" tier means they're going upmarket. Contact sales where there used to be a number means they got burned by self-serve.

Worth checking monthly at minimum.
