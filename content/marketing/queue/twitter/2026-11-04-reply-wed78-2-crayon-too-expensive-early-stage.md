this is exactly why I built KompWatch.

Crayon wants $500+/mo and assumes you have a dedicated CI analyst to manage the firehose. most startups don't have that.

KompWatch is $49/mo. you paste in URLs — pricing page, features page, whatever matters — and you get an email when something changes. no onboarding call, no annual contract, no "book a demo."

might be overkill if you have one competitor. probably right for you if you have 4+.
