[Reply angle: someone complaining their battlecards are always out of date]

The battlecard problem isn't writing. It's freshness.

You can have the best-written competitive doc in the world. If it's 3 months old, your reps will stop trusting it. Once they stop trusting it, they stop reading it.

The update cadence matters more than the format. Weekly beats perfect.
