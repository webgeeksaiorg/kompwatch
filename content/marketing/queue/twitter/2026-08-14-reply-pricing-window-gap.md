The window between a competitor changing their pricing and your sales team knowing about it is usually 2-3 weeks.

That's 2-3 weeks of your reps either over-discounting (didn't know the competitor raised prices) or losing deals (didn't know the competitor dropped below your floor).

That gap is fixable. Set up a watch on their /pricing URL.
