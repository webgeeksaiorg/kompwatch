This is the #1 complaint I hear from people who tried Visualping or similar tools.

The fix isn't fewer alerts — it's smarter classification. When an alert comes in, you need to know immediately: is this a pricing change, a feature announcement, a messaging tweak, or a footer edit?

The last one doesn't matter. The first one does. Big difference.

We score every change 0–100 and classify it by type before it hits your inbox. Still not perfect, but better than "50 alerts, good luck."
