CI research is the one PM task I've seen AI genuinely replace, not augment.

Not because AI is smarter about strategy — it's not. But because the actual work was: check 12 pages, note what changed, write a bullet summary, paste into Slack. That's a script. Has been a script the whole time.

The hard part was always figuring out what actually mattered in the diff.
