The CI workflow that actually works for a 3-person product team:

1. Pick 4-5 pages per competitor. Pricing, features, homepage hero, /alternatives if they have one. Nothing else.
2. Set daily snapshots.
3. When a change fires: classify it yourself. Pricing tweak vs full messaging overhaul — different urgency.
4. Update your battlecard the same day. Not "this sprint." Today.
5. Slack the sales team with one sentence: "Acme dropped their free tier. Here's the new limit."

That's it. No weekly CI review meeting. No 30-page report.

Most teams overcomplicate this because enterprise tools are designed to justify analyst headcount. You don't have an analyst. That's fine.
