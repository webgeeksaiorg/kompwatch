Google Alerts works OK for brand mentions and news. Completely useless for pricing page changes.

It only picks up content Google has indexed. A competitor quietly changing their pricing tier limits, swapping a feature from one plan to another, updating their /vs/ page copy — Google Alerts won't catch any of that.

It's better than nothing for broad keyword monitoring. Just don't rely on it for the stuff that actually affects your sales conversations.
