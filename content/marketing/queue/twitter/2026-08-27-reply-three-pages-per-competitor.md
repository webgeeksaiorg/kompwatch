Start with three pages per competitor:

1. /pricing — did they add/remove tiers, change numbers, run a discount?
2. /changelog or /updates — what shipped? Are they moving toward your space?
3. /careers — what are they hiring for? ML engineers in a "simple tool" is a tell.

Everything else you can check quarterly, not weekly.
