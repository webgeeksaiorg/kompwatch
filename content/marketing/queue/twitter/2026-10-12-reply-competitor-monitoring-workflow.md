Honest answer from doing this manually for 2 years and then building a tool to replace myself:

1. Pick 4-5 pages per competitor max. Pricing + /vs/ pages + changelog. That's it.
2. Ignore the homepage unless they're repositioning.
3. Job listings tell you what they're building 6 months early.
4. Set a specific time to review alerts. Friday EOD works for me.

The mistake is watching too many pages and drowning in noise.
