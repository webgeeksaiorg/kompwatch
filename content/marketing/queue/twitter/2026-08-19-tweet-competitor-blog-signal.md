Hot tip for PMs: your competitor's blog is the best early-warning system you have.

New post about "enterprise security controls" = they're going upmarket.
New post about "5-minute setup" = they're losing deals on complexity.
Radio silence for 6 weeks = something is wrong internally.

Their content team tells the real story. You just have to read it consistently enough to notice.

(We automated this part. But even manually, start reading their blog like it's a signal.)
