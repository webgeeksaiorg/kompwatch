[reply angle: someone arguing email is dead for B2B marketing]

My digest emails (weekly competitor change summaries) open at ~62%.

Not because I'm a great copywriter. Because the content is: "here's exactly what changed on your competitor's site this week."

Utility emails don't die. Newsletter emails die. If the person actually needs the information, they open it.
