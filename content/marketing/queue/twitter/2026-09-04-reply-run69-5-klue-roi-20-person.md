At 20 people: almost certainly not worth it at Crayon/Klue price points.

They're designed for orgs with a dedicated CI analyst to curate the firehose. Without that person, you're paying for noise.

What's worth it at 20 people: something that monitors 10-15 specific pages and emails you when pricing or features change. Total CI budget: $49/mo. That scales to 50 people easily.

The $10K+/year tools earn their price when you have someone whose job is competitive intelligence. Most 20-person startups don't have that yet.
