The competitor feature gap is almost always smaller than your product team thinks.

The gap looks big when you check the competitor's marketing site. Check their changelog instead. Half those "upcoming features" are been half-shipped for 6+ months with 3 customer logos and an asterisk.

Real feature parity research = read changelogs, not feature comparison tables.
