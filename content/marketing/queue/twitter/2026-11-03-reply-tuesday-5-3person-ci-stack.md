What worked for us with 3 people and no CI budget:

1. Pick 3 real competitors (not 15 aspirational ones)
2. List the 4-5 pages that actually matter per competitor
3. Set daily automated screenshots/snapshots
4. Weekly 15-min review of HIGH-severity changes only
5. Update battlecard section for anything that affects active deals

That's it. No dedicated analyst. No $500/mo platform.

The discipline is step 4. Blocking the time. Everything else is tooling.
