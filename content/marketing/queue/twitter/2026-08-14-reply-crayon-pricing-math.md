Crayon's pricing is a real problem for early-stage teams. The ROI math only works if you're already doing enough deals where losing even 5% fewer to competitors covers the cost.

Below ~50 salespeople, the math usually doesn't pencil. Which is weird because those are the teams where a competitor pricing a new tier below yours actually matters most.
