For a team of 5-15 people without a dedicated CI analyst, this is what actually works:

1. Pick 3-5 real competitors (not aspirational ones)
2. Track 4 pages per competitor: pricing, product/features, homepage, jobs
3. Daily or weekly alerts when those pages change
4. Spend 20 min/week reviewing what changed and why

That's it. No 30-tab spreadsheet. No quarterly "competitive landscape" doc that nobody reads.

The hardest part is step 4. Getting the alert is easy. Deciding what to do about it is the actual work.
