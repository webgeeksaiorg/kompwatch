The battlecard itself isn't the problem. It's the update process.

Most teams have no system for it. Someone builds the initial one, it lives in a Notion doc, and then it slowly drifts out of sync with reality. Sales keeps using it because "it's better than nothing."

The fix is monitoring the competitor's pricing and features pages automatically, not scheduling quarterly battlecard reviews that never happen.
