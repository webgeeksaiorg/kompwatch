The PM competitive research loop I see most often:

1. Bookmark competitor pages
2. Check them "when you remember"
3. Remember 2 months later
4. Notice the pricing changed
5. Scramble to figure out when it happened

The alert never comes from the tool. It comes from a sales rep who lost a deal.

That's the actual signal you're optimizing for without realizing it.
