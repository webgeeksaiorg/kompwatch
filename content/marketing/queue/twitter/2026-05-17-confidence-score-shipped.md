Shipped the confidence score today.

Every detected competitor change now gets a 0-100 certainty rating based on what actually changed. Pricing rewrite on the pricing page = 89. One CSS class renamed = 8.

Why this matters: if every alert feels equally urgent, people stop acting on them. The tool becomes noise. This was the exact behavior I was seeing in the old alert emails — everything looked the same, nothing felt actionable.

Now the high-confidence changes sit at the top. The low-confidence ones are still there if you want them, but they don't scream.

Small thing. Changes how the whole tool feels.
