The battlecard problem isn't writing them. It's that they're stale the second you publish.

Competitor drops a new tier. Updates their security page. Adds a G2 award badge. Your battlecard still has old data.

Sales reps figure this out fast and stop trusting them. Then you're back to Slack messages asking "did their pricing change?"

Automating the monitoring half of this is the only fix that scales.
