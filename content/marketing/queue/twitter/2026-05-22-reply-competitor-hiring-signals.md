Job postings are the best leading indicator I've found.

A "Competitive Intelligence Manager" listing means they just started losing deals to you and don't know why. Four ML engineers in one month means an AI feature lands in Q4. Three failed attempts to fill the same senior eng role = team retention problem.

Public information. Most teams never read it this way.
