8 weeks in, 0 paid customers. so take this with that context.

what it has done: made me explain the product in plain language every week. my messaging gets cleaner because I have to write it out loud to humans.

what it hasn't done: directly driven signups I can trace.

my guess: it works as credibility layer for people who find you another way. not as a primary acquisition channel on its own. still figuring this out tbh.
