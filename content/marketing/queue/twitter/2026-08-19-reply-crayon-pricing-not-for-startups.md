Crayon's entry tier starts at $15K/year. Klue is in the same ballpark. Both require a sales call to even see pricing.

That's the market they're optimizing for — enterprise procurement cycles with budget to burn.

If you're a 10-person startup, you're literally not their customer. They'll be polite on the demo, then quote you something that requires a CFO signature.

That's why I built KompWatch. $49/mo, self-serve, no sales call. You paste URLs, it watches them, you get an email when stuff changes.
