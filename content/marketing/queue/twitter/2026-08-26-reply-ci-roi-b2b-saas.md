It depends on your sales cycle.

If a deal is won or lost based on head-to-head comparisons with specific competitors — yeah, worth it. If you're early-stage and still figuring out who your competitors even are, probably not.

The worst outcome is paying $500/mo for CI and having a PM spend 10% of their time distilling it into slides no one reads.

Better version: watch 3-5 competitor pages, act on what you see. Much cheaper. Only upgrade the tool when the watching itself becomes a bottleneck.
