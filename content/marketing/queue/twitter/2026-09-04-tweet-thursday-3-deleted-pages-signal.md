A competitor removing a feature page is more interesting than them adding one.

Adding pages is marketing. Removing them is retreat or pivot.

I've seen teams monitor for new competitor pages and completely miss when /features/collaboration quietly 404'd. That product area was dead. They just hadn't announced it.

Six months later the competitor doubled down on enterprise and dropped SMB entirely. First signal was that deleted page.

What your competitors stop talking about matters.
