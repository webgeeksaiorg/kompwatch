We got our Klue renewal last month. Quote came back 40% higher than year 1.

Their explanation: "additional seats and expanded coverage." We'd added 2 people.

The renewal math on these platforms is wild. Year 1 is a loss leader, year 2 is where they make it back.

Ended up canceling and moving everything to a combination of KompWatch ($49/mo) + a shared Notion doc for analyst commentary. 80% of the value, maybe 2% of the cost.

The 20% we lost was the social listening piece. Honestly don't miss it.
