the workflow I ended up with as a solo PM before I automated it:

- 6 bookmarked competitor pages
- Monday calendar block: "30-min CI audit"
- Google doc with "last seen" dates
- Slack channel #competitors where I'd post screenshots

this worked until it didn't. the problem isn't the system. it's that nothing alerts you when something changes in between your Monday blocks.

that gap is the whole problem.
