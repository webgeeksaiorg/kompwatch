At early stage I'd watch 2-3 competitors max, 4 pages each.

You don't need a Klue subscription. You need to know when their pricing changes and when they add features you don't have. That's the whole job.

It becomes worth automating the moment you have more than 3 competitors to watch. Under that, even a basic change alert is enough.
