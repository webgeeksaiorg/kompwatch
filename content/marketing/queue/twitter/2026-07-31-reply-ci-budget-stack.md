Free tier stack that works:

- Wayback Machine: spot major historical page shifts
- RSS on competitor blogs (most have feeds, nobody checks)
- Their /changelog or /whatsnew page — set a bookmark + reminder
- Their job listings: 3 ML engineer openings tells you what they're building

Automate the website watching part when you have the budget. Until then, Monday morning manual check, 20 mins, 4 URLs. Calendar block it or it won't happen.
