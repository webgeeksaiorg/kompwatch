One thing I'd add: watch what competitors do to their *free tier* more than their paid tiers.

Paid tier changes are marketing. Free tier changes are product strategy. Gutting free = they've figured out conversion. Adding free seats = they're burning to grow.

Both tell you something. One tells you more.
