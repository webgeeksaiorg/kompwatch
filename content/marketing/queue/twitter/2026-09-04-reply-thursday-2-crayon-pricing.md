Crayon is ~$25K/year minimum now. they're not really targeting your company — they're targeting the enterprise team that has a dedicated CI analyst and a $100K enablement budget.

if that's not you, you're paying for features you'll never use and onboarding you'll never finish. there are alternatives that just do the monitoring part for $49/mo.
