Our setup (3 people, no dedicated CI analyst):

1. Track 4-5 pages per competitor: pricing, features, homepage hero, /alternatives or /compare pages
2. Alert fires when content changes
3. AI classifies it: pricing change, feature add, messaging shift, or noise
4. Weekly digest lands in Slack

That's it. Nothing exotic. The classification step is what makes it useful — raw diffs are unreadable.
