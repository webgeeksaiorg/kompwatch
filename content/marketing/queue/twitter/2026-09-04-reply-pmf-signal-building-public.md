One thing nobody tells you about early PMF signals: they're inconclusive for longer than you want.

Users using the product doesn't prove you have PMF. Paid users doesn't prove it either. It starts to feel real when someone emails you upset that something broke — not to cancel, just genuinely annoyed because they rely on it.

Had that email 3 weeks ago. Best day of the year.
