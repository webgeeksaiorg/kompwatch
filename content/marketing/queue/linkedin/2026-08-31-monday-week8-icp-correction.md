Week 7 taught me I was solving the right problem for the wrong person.

I built KompWatch for solo PMs. Got a bunch of signups. Zero paid conversions.

So I looked at engagement data instead of signup data.

The people who opened every email, clicked every link, replied with questions: PMs at Series A/B SaaS companies. 20-100 employees. Someone owns "competitive" as part of their responsibilities. No dedicated analyst. No Crayon budget.

They're not looking for a competitive intelligence *platform*. They want an early warning system.

Specifically: tell me when a competitor changes their pricing page, adds a feature, or shifts their messaging. Send it to my inbox before my next sales cycle. Let me update my battlecard in 20 minutes, not 2 days.

That's the whole job. Not a dashboard. Not a quarterly briefing.

I'd been building landing page copy for the wrong persona for 7 weeks.

The frustrating part? Those users were there from week 1. I just wasn't reading the signal.

Adjusting ICP, onboarding flow, and pricing page copy this week. If you're a PM at a growth-stage SaaS company who owns competitive without an analyst, I want to talk to you.

What does your current "competitive monitoring" actually look like?
