The competitive intelligence process most SaaS teams have:

1. Schedule a Slack reminder to "check competitors" every 2 weeks
2. Someone opens a few websites on a Monday
3. Nothing obvious jumps out
4. Mark it done

The competitive intelligence process most SaaS teams need:

1. Get alerted when something actually changes
2. Read a 3-sentence summary of what changed
3. Decide if it matters

The first version feels like a process. The second one actually works.

I've talked to a lot of product and sales teams about this. The failure mode isn't laziness — it's that manual spot-checks have too many gaps. Wednesday deploys. Weekend pricing updates. A new case study that repositions their messaging.

The thing that changed between the two processes above isn't effort. It's direction. Monitoring for change instead of checking on a schedule.

That's the whole idea behind KompWatch. You paste in URLs, we watch them, you get an email when something shifts. No 30-slide competitive strategy deck required.
