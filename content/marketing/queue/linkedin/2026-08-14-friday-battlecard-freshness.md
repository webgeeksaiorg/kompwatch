The battlecard problem nobody talks about:

You spend 3 hours building a great competitive battlecard. You distribute it to the sales team. It's accurate on day 1.

Six months later, your competitor has:
- Dropped their price by 15%
- Added the feature your reps said you had exclusive
- Changed their ICP language to match your core segment

Your reps are still using the same battlecard.

The battlecard isn't the problem. The update process (or lack of one) is.

Most teams treat battlecards like documentation. You write them once, review them "quarterly," and hope nothing changed in between. The quarterly review doesn't happen. Or it happens and nobody has time to do it properly.

The fix is treating competitor pages like infrastructure you monitor continuously — not a research project you schedule.

Pricing page changed? Sales team knows within 24 hours. Feature comparison table updated? Battlecard author gets pinged automatically.

Still figuring out the workflow for this ourselves. But the "build once, forget" approach was costing us deals.
