3 months of building in public. Still no paying customers.

Here's what I've learned about competitive intelligence from being on both sides of it — as someone who needed it and as someone building a tool for it:

The expensive platforms (Crayon, Klue, $500/mo+) aren't solving a harder problem. They're just more thorough. More sources, more data, more integrations.

Most teams I talk to don't need more data. They need to know within 48 hours when their main competitor changes their pricing page. That's it.

The mismatch I keep seeing: teams that could benefit from basic competitor monitoring aren't doing it at all because the tools are priced for enterprise procurement, not for a PM who checks competitor websites every Monday.

That's the gap KompWatch is trying to fill. Watch what matters. Get emailed when it changes. $49/mo.

Still figuring out how to find the people who need this before they resign themselves to the Monday morning manual tab-check.

If that's you — or if you know that person — I'd genuinely appreciate an introduction. stive@kompwatch.com.
