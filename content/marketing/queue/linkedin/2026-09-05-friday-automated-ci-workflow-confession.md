I have a confession: for two years I tracked 8 competitors manually.

Every Monday. Six browser tabs. A spreadsheet I updated when I remembered to.

The thing I didn't realize until I automated it: I was catching changes *after* they mattered.

The competitor went from self-serve to "Contact Sales" on a Tuesday. I found out the following Monday. By then, three prospects had hit that wall and churned. I had no idea.

Automated monitoring didn't just save me time. It moved the signal from reactive to proactive.

What I actually monitor now (4 pages per competitor, nothing more):
→ Pricing page — plan structure, pricing, CTAs
→ Signup flow — self-serve vs. contact sales gate
→ Features page — new additions, removed free features  
→ Jobs page — what teams they're hiring for (roadmap signal)

The jobs page is underrated. If a competitor posts 4 ML engineer roles in one week, they're building something. You have maybe 6 months.

Still figuring out how to build this sustainably at $49/mo instead of the $500/mo enterprise tools want. But the workflow itself? Worth it from day one.
