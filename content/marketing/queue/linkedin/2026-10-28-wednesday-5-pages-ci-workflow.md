Something I noticed after 6 months of automated competitor monitoring:

The teams that get actual value from CI aren't the ones with the best tools. They're the ones who've narrowed what they track.

I used to watch everything. Every competitor. Every page. Full site crawls.

Result: 40+ alerts per week. I stopped reading them by week 3.

What actually works:

**5 pages per competitor. That's it.**
- Pricing page
- Features or compare page  
- Homepage hero (the above-fold pitch)
- /alternatives or /vs pages if they have them
- Job listings (3+ ML hires = AI feature in 6 months)

**Why job listings?**
Hiring is the most honest signal a company sends. They can't fake headcount. When a competitor posts 4 senior backend engineers in a month after being quiet for 6 months, something is being built. You want 6 months lead time on that, not a surprise at a trade show.

**The CI workflow that sticks:**
1. Alert fires
2. One person classifies it: pricing change vs messaging tweak vs major feature announcement
3. Update the battlecard section same day
4. Slack the sales team one sentence: "Acme changed their free tier limit. New cap is X."

No weekly review meeting. No 30-page report. The quarterly CI review always gets deprioritized. The Slack alert doesn't.

I built KompWatch because the alternative was either a $500/mo enterprise platform or a manual tab-refresh ritual every Monday. Neither scaled.

What pages do you track for competitors? Curious if the job listing signal works for other teams.
