I stopped posting this week and did five customer calls instead.

One question: "How do you find out when a competitor changes something?"

Five calls. Five different answers. Zero of them were good.

The pattern I found:

→ Small teams: manual checks that happen "when someone remembers"
→ Mid-stage teams: Google Alerts (misses actual website changes, catches press coverage only)
→ Growth-stage teams with Crayon/Klue: "the digest goes unread"
→ Everyone: found out about the last major competitor change from a customer

That last one hit hard. Every person I talked to knew what their main competitor's most recent pricing change was. And every single one found out after the fact — from a prospect objecting, a customer asking, or someone internally noticing during a demo.

The gap isn't awareness that competitors exist. It's awareness that competitors change — and when.

The "we have Crayon" teams weren't better off than the "we check manually" teams. They were paying more for the same outcome: late discovery.

What works: automated monitoring of the 5-6 actual pages that matter (pricing, changelog, careers, product, home hero, compare pages), with a weekly digest that's readable in 3 minutes.

That's what I'm building with KompWatch. $49/mo for teams who can't afford $500/mo enterprise contracts and don't want to check manually.

Five calls taught me more about the actual problem than two months of building. If you're in product and want to be one of my next five — DMs open.
