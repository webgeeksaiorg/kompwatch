3 months into building KompWatch. 0 new paid subscribers this month.

Here's what I've actually learned — not the optimistic version:

**What worked:**
The monitoring infrastructure is solid. Headless browser scraping, HTML diffs, AI classification of changes (pricing vs feature vs messaging), confidence scores. That part works.

**What didn't:**
Distribution. Getting the right people to find it and actually use it habitually.

Turns out building the data collection layer is the tractable engineering problem. Getting it embedded in someone's Monday morning workflow is the hard product problem.

**The number that keeps me going:**
42% of pricing pages in our tracked set changed in the last 90 days. Most of those customers would have missed it if they were doing this manually.

That's real. The tool does what it says it does.

Now I have to figure out how to get it in front of the PMs and sales leads who'd actually use it before a deal review.

Still figuring that part out.
