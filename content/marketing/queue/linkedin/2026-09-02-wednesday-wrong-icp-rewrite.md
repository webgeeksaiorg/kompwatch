8 weeks building KompWatch. 0 paid customers. And I just figured out I've been selling to the wrong person.

I built competitor monitoring software for PMMs. PMMs at companies big enough to have PMMs already have Crayon. Or an analyst. Or both. $49/mo is irrelevant to them because it's not their money and they're not the one doing the manual work.

The person I actually built this for: a solo PM or technical founder at a 5–30 person SaaS. 

They're doing competitive research themselves every Monday morning. 90 minutes of manually checking pricing pages and changelog entries. They do it inconsistently because it's boring and there's always something more urgent. They've missed two or three competitor moves in the past year because of it.

That's the person who would pay $49/mo to stop doing the manual version.

Same product. Completely different ICP. Different landing page copy, different subreddits, different cold email angle.

I learned this by watching 47 people trial the product and not convert. The ones who engaged most during onboarding were the solo operators, not the PMMs. I was writing copy for the wrong person.

Rewriting the homepage this week. "Know when your competitors change their pricing. Automatically." Less impressive than "AI-powered competitor monitoring." Probably more useful.

If you're the person I just described — doing CI alone, manually, without a budget for enterprise tools — I'd genuinely love to talk. Not a sales call. Just trying to understand if I'm describing you correctly.
