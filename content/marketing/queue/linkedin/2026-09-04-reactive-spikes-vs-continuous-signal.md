**The most expensive competitor intelligence mistake I see SaaS teams make:**

They treat it as a research project instead of a system.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

Someone on the team gets spooked by a competitor announcement. There's a flurry of activity — everyone digs in, screenshots get shared in Slack, there's an emergency product review meeting.

Two weeks later, the adrenaline is gone and you're back to not watching.

Then three months later, the same competitor quietly ships the feature they announced — and you catch it by accident when a prospect mentions it on a demo call.

This is the pattern I see constantly. **Reactive spikes instead of continuous signal.**

The fix isn't hiring a competitive analyst. It's building a monitoring system that runs without human effort.

What that means practically:
- Automated change detection across competitor websites (not just Google Alerts)
- AI-summarized digests delivered on a schedule, not when someone remembers to check
- Clear severity signals so the team knows when to act vs. when to note and move on

The teams that get this right spend LESS time on competitive intel overall — not more. They just stop losing the signal between the spikes.
