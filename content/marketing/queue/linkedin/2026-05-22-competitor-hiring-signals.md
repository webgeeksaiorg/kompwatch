Something I've learned from watching competitor websites full-time: the job postings page is the most underrated CI signal.

A competitor's pricing page tells you where they are today.

Their hiring tells you where they'll be in 6 months.

Patterns I've seen hold:

→ 4-5 ML engineer postings in one month = AI feature launching in Q3/Q4
→ Cluster of enterprise sales reps = moving upmarket (pricing changes follow)
→ "Competitive Intelligence Manager" listing = they're losing deals to you and don't know why yet
→ Same senior engineering role posted 3 times over 6 months = retention problem, which is a shipping problem
→ Zero open eng roles after 18 months of growth = hiring freeze or they're done

The window is short. Most postings exist for 2–8 weeks. If you're checking quarterly, you're always catching up to signals that are already months old.

We added careers page monitoring to KompWatch exactly for this. The AI summary reads: "Added: Senior ML Engineer (3), Staff Infrastructure Engineer (1). Removed: Marketing Coordinator (2)." Takes 30 seconds to read. Saves the quarterly review tab-check entirely.

Pricing shows you where they are. Hiring shows you where they're going. Both are worth watching.
