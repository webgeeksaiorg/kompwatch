Three CI tools have exited the SMB market in 18 months.

Crayon repriced to $10K+ minimum. Klue followed. Now Kompyte just repositioned as enterprise-only — previously available as a $99/mo Semrush add-on, now behind a sales process with enterprise pricing.

The pattern is real: VC-backed CI platforms optimize for $50K-$200K ACV contracts because the math works out better. SMB churn is high, support cost is proportionally higher, and the contracts don't move needles in board meetings.

So what happens to the product teams, sales leaders, and founders at 20-200 person companies who actually need this?

They go back to Google Alerts and manually checking websites on Monday mornings.

I'm biased — I built KompWatch for exactly this gap. $49/mo, no sales call, cancel anytime. But even setting that aside: there's a structural problem here. The enterprise consolidation keeps creating displaced users with no real alternative at their budget.

If you're a Kompyte user who got the enterprise repricing notice and you're looking for alternatives — I'd genuinely recommend evaluating a few options at this tier before just canceling and going manual:

1. KompWatch — AI summaries, headless browser, website-focused
2. Visualping — simpler, good for visual changes
3. changedetection.io — open source, self-hosted if you want full control

Happy to answer questions about the space, KompWatch or otherwise.
