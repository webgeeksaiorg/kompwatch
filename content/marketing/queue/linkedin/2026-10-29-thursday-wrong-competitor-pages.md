Most product teams watch the wrong competitor pages.

Everyone pins their competitor's homepage. Maybe the pricing page.

But the homepage is marketing. It changes slowly, and the changes are designed to obscure strategy.

The pages that tell you what's actually happening:

**1. The changelog / release notes**
They ship. They update the changelog. They can't hide it — customers need to know. This is unfiltered product roadmap signal.

**2. The jobs page**
Hiring 3 backend engineers with "experience in data pipelines"? They're building something. Hiring a Head of Enterprise Sales? Their PLG motion is stalling. The jobs page leaks strategy constantly.

**3. The /compare pages**
How they position against you. What objections they're preemptively answering. Watch this page and you're watching their sales conversations in real time.

**4. The /pricing page**
Obviously. But most teams check it manually once a quarter. By then the change is 6 weeks old and their sales team already adjusted.

We built KompWatch to watch these pages automatically — snapshot comparison + AI digest that explains what changed in plain English.

The question isn't whether your competitors are moving. They are. The question is whether you find out this week or next quarter.

---
The most surprising thing we learned building this: our best users watch 8–12 pages per competitor, not just the homepage. The compound value of watching the right pages is much higher than watching everything.
