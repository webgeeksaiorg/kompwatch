I used to spend about 2 hours a week checking competitor websites manually.

6 tabs. Pricing pages. Feature pages. /alternatives pages where they talk about why they're better than everyone else.

Half the time nothing changed. Quarter of the time something had changed and I'd already missed it. I'd find out from a prospect asking about a feature I didn't know the competitor had just added.

That's the hidden cost nobody talks about — not the 2 hours, but the decisions you make based on stale information.

Built KompWatch to fix this for myself first. Cron job that snapshots pages and diffs them. Email when something changes. Plain English summary of what's different.

The tool is real and the problem is real. But what I keep underestimating is how many teams have just accepted the manual process as "just how it is."

If you're still doing this manually — what would it take to change that?
