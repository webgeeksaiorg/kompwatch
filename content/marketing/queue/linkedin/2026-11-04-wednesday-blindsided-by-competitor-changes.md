Most product teams learn about competitor changes from their own customers.

A prospect mentions it on a call. A churned user cites it in an exit survey. Someone in Slack posts a screenshot weeks after it happened.

That's not competitive intelligence. That's playing catch-up.

The irony is that most of what actually matters is public. Pricing pages. Feature updates. Job listings. These aren't secrets — they're just easy to miss when you're heads-down building.

I built KompWatch because I was tired of being the person who found out last. We monitor competitor pages automatically and send a weekly digest of what changed, explained in plain English. No analyst required.

The goal isn't to react to everything. It's to stop being blindsided.

If your team is still doing this manually — or not doing it at all — it's worth asking what it's cost you so far.
