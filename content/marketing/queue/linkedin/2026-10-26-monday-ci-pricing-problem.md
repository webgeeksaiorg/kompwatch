The competitive intelligence industry has a pricing problem.

Crayon: starts at $15K/year.
Klue: starts at $15K/year.
Kompyte: enterprise pricing, no number listed.

Then there's what most product teams actually need: "tell me when a competitor changes their pricing page."

I know because I was one of those product teams. Six browser tabs pinned. Every Monday morning I'd go through them manually. Took 45 minutes. Half the time I'd forget anyway, or the change had happened on a Tuesday and I'd missed the context.

So I built KompWatch. Paste URLs, watch specific pages, get an email digest when something changes — written in plain English by AI. Not a 30-slide battlecard. Just: "Acme Co removed their $29/mo tier and now starts at $79/mo."

$49/mo. No annual contract, no 45-minute demo call.

The enterprise CI platforms serve a real need — if you have a dedicated CI analyst and a large sales team that needs battlecards, they're worth it.

But for a 5-person SaaS team that just wants to know what their 4 main competitors are doing? There's a different tool for that now.

We don't do social listening. We don't do patent tracking. We watch websites and tell you what changed. That's it.

Curious how other product teams handle this — do you use a tool, or is it still manual?
