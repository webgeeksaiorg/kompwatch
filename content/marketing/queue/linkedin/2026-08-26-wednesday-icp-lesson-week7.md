7 weeks building KompWatch in public.

Still 0 paid subscribers. I want to be honest about what I'm learning.

I've been writing content for product marketers. PMMs, CI analysts, teams with dedicated competitive intel functions. That's a real market — but it's also a market that already has Crayon and Klue serving it.

This week I had 3 calls with people using the free tier. None of them fit the PMM profile. Two were PMs who do competitive research as a side responsibility. One was a solo founder checking on two competitors.

They all said the same thing: "I just want to know when something changes. I don't need analysis. I don't need battlecards."

That's a different buyer than who I've been writing for.

The lesson from 6 weeks of zero conversions isn't that the product is wrong. It's that I've been writing to an audience that's already paying $20K/year for something I don't replace.

The audience that needs what I actually built is the PM who opens 4 browser tabs on Monday morning. They're harder to find. But they exist.

Still figuring out how to reach them.
