It's Monday, which used to mean 90 minutes of manual browser tab hell.

12 competitors. Same 3 pages each. Pricing. Features. Blog. In and out, hoping I hadn't missed anything in the past week.

I built KompWatch to kill that ritual.

Not because I'm lazy — because I was missing things anyway. A pricing change that went live on a Thursday. A new case study that signaled an enterprise push. A job listing that told me exactly which problem they were trying to solve.

Manual checks don't catch those. Weekly digests do.

35 days since launch. 0 paid subscribers. Still figuring out who needs this badly enough to pay for it vs. just tolerating the tab routine.

But I know the tab routine costs real time. And usually costs real information too.

If you're a PMM or founder who still does this manually on Mondays — I'd genuinely love to know what your setup looks like. Not pitching, just curious.

Happy Monday.
