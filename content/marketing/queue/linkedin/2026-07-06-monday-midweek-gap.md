Something I wish I'd understood earlier:

The competitor monitoring problem isn't Monday morning. It's Wednesday afternoon.

For two years I had 6 browser tabs pinned — one for each competitor's pricing page. Monday morning ritual. Cup of coffee, open tabs, scan for changes.

Felt thorough. Wasn't.

The gap: competitors don't change their pricing on Monday so you can catch it. A quiet price drop, a new annual plan, a feature tier shift — these happen whenever. Mid-week. Friday night. Doesn't matter.

The week I got blindsided on a demo was a Thursday change. Found out Tuesday from a prospect who said "your competitor is cheaper now."

That's when I built KompWatch. Automated watchers on pricing, feature, and compare pages. Digest in my inbox when something changes — not on a schedule, on a trigger.

30 days in. Free tier is getting signups. Pro hasn't converted yet (0 paid subs, building in public so I'll say it).

But the product itself works. My competitor research is now: open digest, read what changed, update battlecard if needed. 15 minutes instead of tabs.

The monitoring problem is solved. The conversion problem is next.

What's your current competitor monitoring workflow?
