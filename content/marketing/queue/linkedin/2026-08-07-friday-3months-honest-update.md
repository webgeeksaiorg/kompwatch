End of week update on building KompWatch (honest version):

3 months in. 0 paid subscribers.

But I've been watching 15+ SaaS competitor pricing pages every day for 3 months, and here's what I actually know now that I didn't before:

**42% of them changed their pricing page in a 90-day window.**

Most weren't announced. No blog post. No email to customers. One day the page was one thing, next day something different.

The companies that caught it responded. The ones that didn't found out in a lost deal debrief.

That's what competitor monitoring actually solves — not "understanding the competitive landscape." It's much more tactical than that. It's: did something change that would affect how I pitch tomorrow?
