7 weeks. 47 trial signups. 0 paid.

Friday update, because I said I'd share this stuff honestly.

The week 7 lesson: I was targeting the wrong person.

Built KompWatch for PMMs doing competitive intelligence at growth-stage companies. Turns out those people already have Crayon. Or an analyst. Or both.

The person who actually needs what I built: solo PM or founder at a 5-30 person SaaS who checks competitor websites manually every Monday morning and has been doing it for months because no tool fits their budget and workflow.

That's a different person. Different job title. Different LinkedIn feed. Probably not reading this.

Changing the messaging. Keeping the product.

One thing that moved this week: rewrote the onboarding first step. "Add your first competitor" → "Paste your competitor's pricing page URL." Trial-to-first-alert dropped from 3.1 days to 18 hours. Not a paid conversion. But the right direction.

Still here. Still building.
