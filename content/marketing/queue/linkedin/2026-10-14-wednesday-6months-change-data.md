6 months of running KompWatch in production. Here's what the data actually shows about how competitor websites change.

Pricing pages: edited roughly once every 6–8 weeks on average. But the distribution is not uniform — 40%+ of edits happen in the last week of a quarter. End-of-quarter pricing tweaks are real.

Features pages: more frequent, but mostly low-signal. Copy changes, reordering, minor additions. The high-signal moves (killing a tier, adding a new plan, dropping free) are rare but happen about every 10–12 weeks per competitor.

Job postings: most underrated signal. Teams don't post infra engineering roles unless something is coming. Tracked a competitor go from 1 to 6 infra job listings over 45 days. New product tier shipped 3 months later. You can see it before it ships.

What doesn't change much: homepage hero copy. Everyone thinks competitors are constantly A/B testing their messaging. Mostly they're not. The homepage sits for months. Check it quarterly, not daily.

The "I need to monitor everything" instinct is the enemy of good CI. Pick the pages that actually tell you something. Watch them consistently. The Monday morning tab ritual isn't the problem — it's that you're checking the wrong tabs.
