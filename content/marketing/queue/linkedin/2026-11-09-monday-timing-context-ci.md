There's a specific kind of Monday morning dread that comes from checking a competitor's website and realizing something changed — and you don't know when.

Was it last Tuesday? Three weeks ago? Before that big renewal meeting?

It's not just about the change itself. It's the uncertainty around timing. If you don't know when it happened, you can't connect it to anything — the demo that went sideways, the customer who asked an uncomfortable question, the renewal that underperformed.

Timing context is what turns a competitor update from trivia into intelligence.

This is the thing automated monitoring solves that manual tab-checking can't: a timestamped record of exactly when every change happened. So when something comes up in a deal conversation, you can say "that happened three weeks ago, here's what changed and here's what it means" rather than "I think I saw something about that recently."

We built change history into KompWatch for this exact reason. Every snapshot diff is timestamped. You can scroll back and see the state of a competitor's pricing page on any given week.

Small thing, but it changes how you think about CI — from "catching up" to "having context."
