A pricing page tells you more than most analyst reports.

I used to work at a company that paid $500/mo for a competitive intelligence platform. The reports were long. Full of market sizing charts and analyst commentary.

What actually drove decisions in Slack: "wait, did they change their pricing page?"

That's it. That was the high-signal thing. Everything else was noise we convinced ourselves was insight.

Now I build KompWatch — a tool that watches competitor websites and emails you when something changes. $49/mo. No 30-slide quarterly briefing.

Things that have actually changed sales conversations when caught early:
→ A competitor quietly removed their startup discount
→ A competitor changed their homepage from "for developers" to "for teams"
→ A competitor added SOC 2 to their pricing page (started selling upmarket)

None of these showed up in any report. All of them showed up as a website change.

What signals do you actually use? Genuinely curious what moves the needle for product/sales teams.
