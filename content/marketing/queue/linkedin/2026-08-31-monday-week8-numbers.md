Week 8 of building in public. Here's what the numbers actually look like.

I adjusted the ICP last week. Solo PMs → PMs at 20-100 person SaaS companies who own "competitive" without analyst support.

Three things changed immediately:

**Email open rate:** 34% → 51%
The wrong ICP will read your emails, just not enough of them.

**Reply rate to cold outreach:** 2% → 11%
I changed one thing: stopped talking about "competitive intelligence" and started talking about "knowing the day your competitor changes their pricing page."

**Sales call show rate:** 60% → 85%
The old persona booked calls and ghosted. The new one shows up because the problem is costing them something real.

Same product. Same pricing ($49/mo Pro). Different person.

I could have found this in week 2 if I'd looked at who was clicking instead of who was signing up. Signups lie. Click behavior tells the truth.
